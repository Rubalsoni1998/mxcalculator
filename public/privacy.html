Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Your numbers stay on your device

Every calculation runs in your browser. The values you type into a calculator are not sent to our servers.

Saved inputs

To save you retyping, calculators remember the last values you entered. They are stored only in your browser (local storage) and never leave your device. Use a calculator's Clear button or clear your browser's site data to remove them. Share links contain the values in the web address, so anyone you send a link to can see those values.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which calculators are popular. It may set cookies and collect technical information such as pages viewed, device type, browser and approximate location. See Google's privacy policy for details.

Our own visit statistics

We also count visits on our own server. For each page view we record the page, time, your IP address, browser and device type, screen size, time zone, language, the site you came from, and how long the page stayed open. A first-party cookie (mx_vid, up to 2 years) lets us tell repeat visitors apart. This data is used only to understand traffic and keep the site running, and is not sold or shared. The numbers you type into calculators are never part of it.

Third-party resources

Pages may load fonts and chart code from third-party content delivery networks, which can receive your IP address when they serve those files.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
