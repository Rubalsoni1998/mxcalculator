Local sales tax in Connecticut

Tax Foundation reports no local general sales tax in Connecticut, so the 6.35% state rate is the general rate everywhere in the state. Some items, such as meals, lodging or vehicles, can be taxed differently.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the sales tax rate in Connecticut?

The Connecticut state sales tax rate is 6.35%. Including local taxes, the average combined rate is 6.35%, which ranks 33 among the 50 states (1 is highest), according to Tax Foundation midyear 2026 data.

Does Connecticut have local sales tax?

Tax Foundation reports no local general sales tax in Connecticut, so the 6.35% state rate is the general rate everywhere in the state. Some items, such as meals, lodging or vehicles, can be taxed differently.

How much is sales tax on $1,000 in Connecticut?

At the state rate of 6.35%, sales tax on $1,000 is $63.50. At the average combined rate of 6.35%, it is $63.50. Your actual amount depends on your local rate.

Is the sales tax in Connecticut higher than the national average?

The average combined rate in Connecticut is 6.35%, which is 1.18 points below the 7.53% national average used by Tax Foundation.
