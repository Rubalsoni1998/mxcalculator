Local sales tax in New Jersey

The state rate in New Jersey is 6.63%. The negative average local figure reflects Urban Enterprise Zones in some cities, where sellers collect half the statewide rate. Most purchases are taxed at the state rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the sales tax rate in New Jersey?

The New Jersey state sales tax rate is 6.63%. Including local taxes, the average combined rate is 6.60%, which ranks 30 among the 50 states (1 is highest), according to Tax Foundation midyear 2026 data.

Does New Jersey have local sales tax?

The state rate in New Jersey is 6.63%. The negative average local figure reflects Urban Enterprise Zones in some cities, where sellers collect half the statewide rate. Most purchases are taxed at the state rate.

How much is sales tax on $1,000 in New Jersey?

At the state rate of 6.63%, sales tax on $1,000 is $66.30. At the average combined rate of 6.60%, it is $66.00. Your actual amount depends on your local rate.

Is the sales tax in New Jersey higher than the national average?

The average combined rate in New Jersey is 6.60%, which is 0.93 points below the 7.53% national average used by Tax Foundation.
