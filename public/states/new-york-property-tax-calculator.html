Getting a more exact estimate

  • Look up your property's assessed value and local levy on your county assessor's website.
  • Subtract any exemption (for example a homestead exemption) in the calculator above; eligibility rules differ by state and county.
  • Remember that a recent sale or a reassessment can raise your bill. New buyers often pay more than the state average suggests.
  • Include property tax in your monthly budget with the mortgage calculator.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the property tax rate in New York?

The average effective property tax rate in New York is 1.30% of home value, which ranks 11 among the 50 states (1 is highest). Local rates vary by county, city and school district.

How much is property tax on a $485,932 home in New York?

At the state average effective rate of 1.30%, property tax on a $485,932 home is about $6,317 a year, or $526 a month.

Is property tax high in New York?

The 1.30% effective rate in New York is above the middle of the pack of the 50 states. For comparison, the simple average across states is 0.90%.

How is property tax calculated in New York?

Local governments multiply the assessed value of your property by their tax rates (often expressed in mills, or dollars per $1,000). The effective rate is the tax bill divided by the home's market value, which makes states easier to compare.
