Local sales tax in Montana

Montana has no statewide sales tax, and Tax Foundation reports no local general sales tax either, so most purchases carry no general sales tax. Other taxes can still apply to specific items such as lodging, meals or vehicles, and some localities add special taxes, so check with the state revenue department for your situation.
