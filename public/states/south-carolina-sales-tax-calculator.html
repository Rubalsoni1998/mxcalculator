Local sales tax in South Carolina

Cities, counties and special districts in South Carolina can add their own sales tax on top of the 6.00% state rate. Tax Foundation puts the average local rate at 1.49%, which makes the average combined rate 7.49%. Your real rate depends on where the sale is sourced, so use the exact combined rate for your address if you know it.
