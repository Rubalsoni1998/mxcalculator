How to use this table

  • The combined figure is an average. The rate where you shop can be higher or lower.
  • Four states have no general sales tax: Delaware, Montana, New Hampshire and Oregon.
  • Some states allow local taxes while others set one statewide rate.
  • For a purchase in any state, use the sales tax calculator; for vehicles, see the car sales tax calculator.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which state has the highest sales tax?

By average combined state and local rate, Louisiana is highest at 10.13% in Tax Foundation's midyear 2026 data.

Which states have no sales tax?

Delaware, Montana, New Hampshire and Oregon have no statewide or general local sales tax. Alaska has no state sales tax but allows local taxes.

What is the national average sales tax rate?

Tax Foundation reports a population-weighted national average combined rate of 7.53% for midyear 2026.
