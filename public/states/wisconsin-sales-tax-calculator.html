Local sales tax in Wisconsin

Cities, counties and special districts in Wisconsin can add their own sales tax on top of the 5.00% state rate. Tax Foundation puts the average local rate at 0.72%, which makes the average combined rate 5.72%. Your real rate depends on where the sale is sourced, so use the exact combined rate for your address if you know it.
