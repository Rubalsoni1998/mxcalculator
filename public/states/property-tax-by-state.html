Reading the table

  • Effective rates are averages. Counties and school districts within a state can differ a lot.
  • A high rate on a low-value home can mean a smaller bill than a low rate on an expensive home, which is why the last column matters.
  • Exemptions, caps and reassessment rules change what you actually pay.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which state has the highest property taxes?

By effective rate, Illinois is highest at 1.88% in the data used here (Tax Foundation 2024).

Which state has the lowest property taxes?

Hawaii has the lowest effective rate in this data at 0.29%.

What is an effective property tax rate?

It is the annual property tax paid divided by the home's market value. It makes states comparable even when their assessment methods differ.
