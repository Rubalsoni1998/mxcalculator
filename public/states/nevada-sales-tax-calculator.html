Local sales tax in Nevada

Cities, counties and special districts in Nevada can add their own sales tax on top of the 6.85% state rate. Tax Foundation puts the average local rate at 1.39%, which makes the average combined rate 8.24%. Your real rate depends on where the sale is sourced, so use the exact combined rate for your address if you know it.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the sales tax rate in Nevada?

The Nevada state sales tax rate is 6.85%. Including local taxes, the average combined rate is 8.24%, which ranks 13 among the 50 states (1 is highest), according to Tax Foundation midyear 2026 data.

Does Nevada have local sales tax?

Cities, counties and special districts in Nevada can add their own sales tax on top of the 6.85% state rate. Tax Foundation puts the average local rate at 1.39%, which makes the average combined rate 8.24%. Your real rate depends on where the sale is sourced, so use the exact combined rate for your address if you know it.

How much is sales tax on $1,000 in Nevada?

At the state rate of 6.85%, sales tax on $1,000 is $68.50. At the average combined rate of 8.24%, it is $82.40. Your actual amount depends on your local rate.

Is the sales tax in Nevada higher than the national average?

The average combined rate in Nevada is 8.24%, which is 0.71 points above the 7.53% national average used by Tax Foundation.
