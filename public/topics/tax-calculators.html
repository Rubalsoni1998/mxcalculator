US Tax Calculators

Federal, state and sales tax tools for 2026

Maintained by the Mx Calculator editorial team · Last updated November 23, 2026

Taxes are easier to plan when you can see how brackets, credits and deductions fit together. Use these calculators to estimate your federal tax, see how credits change your refund, and look up sales and property tax for any state.

Federal income tax

  • Federal Income Tax CalculatorEstimate your federal income tax, effective rate and marginal bracket using current IRS brackets and the standard or itemized deduction.
  • Tax Bracket CalculatorFind your marginal and effective federal tax rate with 2026 and 2025 IRS brackets, tax owed in each bracket and income left before the next one.
  • Federal Tax Refund EstimatorEstimate your federal refund for tax years 2026 and 2025 with IRS brackets, the standard deduction, Child Tax Credit and new tips and overtime deductions.

Credits

  • Earned Income Tax Credit (EITC) CalculatorEstimate your Earned Income Tax Credit with 2026 IRS amounts by filing status, children, earned income and AGI, with a phase-in and phase-out chart.
  • Child Tax Credit CalculatorEstimate your 2026 Child Tax Credit of up to $2,200 per child, the refundable portion (up to $1,700), income phase-outs and your refund.

Investments and windfalls

  • Capital Gains Tax CalculatorEstimate short-term and long-term capital gains tax with 2026 IRS rates, the 3.8% net investment income tax and state tax, and see profit after tax.
  • Lottery Tax Calculator (Powerball / Mega Millions)See what you keep from a Powerball or Mega Millions jackpot, as a lump sum or annuity, after 2026 federal brackets and state tax.

Sales and property tax

  • Sales Tax CalculatorAdd sales tax to a price, or work backwards from a total to find the pre-tax price and the tax amount, for any rate you enter.
  • Car Sales Tax CalculatorCalculate car sales tax with 2026 rates for all 50 states, including trade-in credit rules, local tax and special state rules.
  • Property Tax CalculatorEstimate annual and monthly property tax using US state effective rates, a local rate or mill rate, with exemptions and a 10-year projection.

Tips

  • Your marginal rate applies only to the income in the top bracket, not to all of it.
  • Credits reduce tax dollar for dollar, so they are usually worth more than deductions of the same size.
  • These are estimates. Use IRS guidance or a tax professional for filing decisions.
