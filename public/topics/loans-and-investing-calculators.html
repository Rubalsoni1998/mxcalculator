Loan & Investing Calculators

Borrow smarter and plan how money grows

Maintained by the Mx Calculator editorial team · Last updated November 23, 2026

Whether you are comparing loan offers or planning for retirement, the same ideas apply: interest, time and regular payments. These calculators show how those inputs change the result.

Loans and payments

  • Loan CalculatorCalculate the monthly payment, total interest and amortization schedule for personal, auto or student loans, with extra payment options.
  • Payment CalculatorEstimate the monthly payment on any fixed-rate loan, with total interest and a full amortization schedule.
  • Amortization CalculatorSee a payment-by-payment amortization schedule with principal, interest and remaining balance, and test how extra payments shorten your loan.
  • Auto Loan CalculatorEstimate your monthly car payment with down payment, trade-in, sales tax and fees, and see the total interest you will pay over the loan.
  • Car Affordability CalculatorFind the car price you can afford using the 20/4/10 rule, a percent of income or a payment you choose, with 2026 auto loan rates by credit score.
  • Interest Rate CalculatorFind the annual interest rate (APR) of a loan from the loan amount, term and monthly payment, and see the total interest you will pay.

Saving and investing

  • Compound Interest CalculatorSee how savings grow with regular deposits and daily, monthly or yearly compounding, with the final balance and total interest earned.
  • Investment CalculatorProject the future value of an investment with regular contributions, compounding and an inflation adjustment.
  • Dividend CalculatorProject dividend income, DRIP reinvestment, dividend growth, taxes and portfolio value year by year over any time period.
  • Retirement CalculatorFind out whether your savings will last, how much you need to retire, and how much more to save each year, with 401(k) and IRA contributions.

Interest and inflation

  • Interest CalculatorCalculate simple or compound interest on savings or loans and see total interest earned or owed over any period.
  • Inflation CalculatorSee how inflation changes the buying power of money, and what a price today could cost in the future at the inflation rate you choose.
  • Finance CalculatorSolve time value of money problems: present value, future value, payment, interest rate or number of periods.

Income

  • Salary CalculatorConvert pay between hourly, daily, weekly, bi-weekly, monthly and annual amounts, adjusting for hours, holidays and vacation.

Tips

  • Compare loans by total interest as well as monthly payment.
  • Small rate differences compound over long terms.
  • Test investment plans with a range of returns, not a single guess.
