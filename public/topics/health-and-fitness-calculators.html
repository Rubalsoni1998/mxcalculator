Health & Fitness Calculators

Body measures, nutrition, training and pregnancy

Maintained by the Mx Calculator editorial team · Last updated November 23, 2026

These calculators help you understand body measurements, set calorie and nutrition targets, plan training and track pregnancy dates. They give estimates for general information and do not replace advice from a clinician.

Body measurements

  • BMI CalculatorEnter height and weight in pounds and inches or metric to get your BMI, weight category and the healthy weight range for your height.
  • Healthy Weight CalculatorSee the weight range that is healthy for your height, based on the normal BMI range of 18.5 to 24.9, in pounds or kilograms.
  • Ideal Weight CalculatorGet your ideal body weight by height and sex using the Devine, Robinson, Miller and Hamwi formulas, in pounds or kilograms, side by side.
  • Overweight CalculatorFind out how many pounds or kilograms you are above the healthy BMI range for your height and how much it would take to reach it.
  • Body Fat CalculatorEstimate body fat percentage with the US Navy tape method or BMI, and compare your result with healthy ranges for your age and sex.
  • Army Body Fat CalculatorUse the US Army tape test (neck, waist, hips) to estimate body fat and check whether you meet the standard for your age and sex.
  • Lean Body Mass CalculatorEstimate lean body mass and fat mass from height, weight and sex using the Boer, James and Hume formulas.
  • Body Type CalculatorFind your body shape (apple, pear, hourglass or rectangle) from bust, waist and hip measurements and get your waist-to-hip ratio.
  • Body Surface Area CalculatorCalculate body surface area in m² from height and weight using the Mosteller, DuBois, Haycock and other formulas used in clinical dosing.

Energy and nutrition

  • BMR CalculatorEstimate calories burned at rest with the Mifflin-St Jeor, Harris-Benedict or Katch-McArdle equation, then see what it means for your daily needs.
  • TDEE CalculatorEstimate your total daily energy expenditure and the calories to eat to lose, maintain or gain weight, based on your activity level.
  • Calorie CalculatorFind how many calories you need per day to maintain, lose or gain weight, based on your age, height, weight and activity level.
  • Calories Burned CalculatorEstimate calories burned walking, running, cycling, swimming and more, using MET values and your body weight and workout time.
  • Macro CalculatorGet daily grams of protein, carbohydrates and fat for weight loss, maintenance or muscle gain based on your calorie needs.
  • Protein CalculatorFind how many grams of protein you need each day for general health, weight loss, muscle gain or athletic training.
  • Carbohydrate CalculatorFind how many grams of carbohydrates to eat each day for weight loss, maintenance or gain, based on your daily calorie needs.
  • Fat Intake CalculatorWork out how many grams of total and saturated fat to eat each day for your calorie level and goals.

Training

  • Target Heart Rate CalculatorFind your maximum heart rate and training zones by age using the standard and Karvonen heart rate reserve methods.
  • One Rep Max CalculatorEstimate the heaviest weight you can lift once from a lighter set using the Epley, Brzycki and other formulas, with training percentages.
  • Pace CalculatorFind your running pace per mile or kilometer, finish time or distance for a 5K, 10K, half marathon, marathon or custom run.

Health checks

  • BAC CalculatorEstimate your blood alcohol content (BAC) from weight, sex, drinks and hours, and see roughly how long until it reaches zero. Never drive after drinking.
  • GFR CalculatorEstimate your glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) from serum creatinine, age and sex with the CKD-EPI equation and see the kidney disease stage.

Pregnancy and cycles

  • Pregnancy CalculatorFind your due date, current week, trimester and key milestones from your last period, conception date or IVF date.
  • Due Date CalculatorEstimate your baby’s due date from your last menstrual period, conception date or IVF transfer date, and see how far along you are.
  • Conception CalculatorEstimate when you conceived from your due date or last menstrual period, and see the most likely conception window and fertile days.
  • Conception CalculatorWork out your due date from a conception date, or estimate the conception date from a due date or pregnancy week.
  • Ovulation CalculatorPredict your ovulation day and fertile window from your cycle length and last period, with a calendar of your most fertile days.
  • Period CalculatorPredict your next period dates and see a calendar of upcoming cycles from your last period and average cycle length.
  • Pregnancy Weight Gain CalculatorGet recommended total and week-by-week pregnancy weight gain for singleton or twin pregnancies, based on your pre-pregnancy BMI.

Tips

  • Use BMI as a screening number, then look at waist size, fitness and lab results.
  • Start calorie targets from your TDEE and adjust based on real results.
  • Talk to your doctor before major changes to diet, exercise or pregnancy care.
