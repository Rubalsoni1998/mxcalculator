Home Buying Calculators

Payments, costs and taxes for US buyers

Maintained by the Mx Calculator editorial team · Last updated November 23, 2026

Buying a home involves more than the price. The monthly payment, mortgage insurance, closing costs and property tax all affect what you can afford. These calculators let you test each piece with your own numbers before you talk to a lender.

Estimate the payment

  • Mortgage CalculatorEstimate your monthly mortgage payment with property tax, insurance, PMI and HOA, view the amortization schedule and see how extra payments cut interest.
  • Amortization CalculatorSee a payment-by-payment amortization schedule with principal, interest and remaining balance, and test how extra payments shorten your loan.
  • PMI CalculatorEstimate monthly private mortgage insurance, your total payment with PMI, and when PMI can be removed at 80% and 78% loan-to-value.

Plan the upfront and ongoing costs

  • Closing Cost CalculatorEstimate closing costs for a US home purchase: lender fees, title and government charges, prepaids, FHA or VA fees and total cash to close.
  • Property Tax CalculatorEstimate annual and monthly property tax using US state effective rates, a local rate or mill rate, with exemptions and a 10-year projection.

Investment property

  • Cap Rate CalculatorCalculate cap rate, net operating income, expense ratio and gross rent multiplier for a rental property, or find value from a target cap rate.
  • Capital Gains Tax CalculatorEstimate short-term and long-term capital gains tax with 2026 IRS rates, the 3.8% net investment income tax and state tax, and see profit after tax.

Tips

  • Start with the total monthly payment, not just principal and interest: add property tax, insurance and PMI.
  • Run the numbers with a rate 0.5 to 1 point higher than today to see whether the budget still works.
  • Compare property tax across states on the property tax by state page.
