About the Lean Body Mass Calculator

Lean body mass (LBM) is everything in your body that is not fat: muscle, bones, organs and water. Tracking it shows whether weight loss is coming from fat or from muscle.

This calculator estimates your LBM and fat mass from your height, weight and gender using several commonly used formulas.

How to Use the Lean Body Mass Calculator

  1. Select your gender.
  2. Enter your height and weight.
  3. Click Calculate to see lean body mass from each formula.

Formulas for Lean Body Mass

If you know your body fat percentage: LBM = weight × (1 − body fat %). Without it, regression formulas such as Boer, James and Hume estimate LBM from height and weight, with separate versions for men and women.

Example: Someone who weighs 80 kg with 20 percent body fat has 80 × 0.80 = 64 kg of lean mass and 16 kg of fat.

Why Lean Mass Matters

  • It drives your resting metabolic rate.
  • It supports strength, mobility and healthy ageing.
  • It helps set protein and calorie targets.

Worked Example: Boer Formula

For a man who is 70 in (178 cm) and 180 lb (81.6 kg), the Boer formula (0.407 × weight + 0.267 × height − 19.2) gives a lean body mass of about 61.5 kg (136 lb). The rest, about 20.1 kg, is estimated fat mass, or roughly 25% of body weight.

Using Lean Mass

  • Protein targets are often set per kilogram of lean mass. See the protein calculator.
  • Katch-McArdle BMR uses lean mass directly; see the BMR calculator.
  • Track change over time rather than one reading.

Accuracy

These formulas estimate from height, weight and sex, so they miss differences in muscle and bone. DEXA scans and other clinical methods measure more directly. Use the result as a rough guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is lean body mass the same as muscle mass?

No. LBM includes muscle, bone, organs and body water; muscle is only one part.

How can I increase lean body mass?

Strength training, enough protein, adequate calories and good sleep.

Which formula is most accurate?

They give similar values for average builds. Measured body fat gives better accuracy than any estimate.

Can lean mass go up while weight stays the same?

Yes, when you build muscle and lose fat at the same time.

What is lean body mass?

It is everything in your body except fat: muscle, bone, organs and water.

How do I increase lean body mass?

Resistance training, enough protein and adequate calories and sleep support muscle gain. Results vary by person.

Is lean body mass the same as muscle mass?

No. Lean body mass includes muscle plus bone, organs and body water.
