About the Date Calculator

The date calculator answers two common questions: how long is it between two dates, and what date will it be after adding or subtracting a period of time.

Use it for deadlines, project planning, pregnancies, travel, contracts and countdowns.

How to Use the Date Calculator

  1. Choose Date difference or Add/Subtract.
  2. Enter the dates or the amount of time to add.
  3. Click Calculate to see the result in days, weeks, months and years.

How Date Math Works

Dates are converted to day numbers, so differences account for different month lengths and leap years. Adding months moves to the same day in a later month, or the last day if it does not exist.

Example: From January 1 to December 25, 2026 is 358 days, or 51 weeks and 1 day.

Worked Examples

Days between dates. From January 1, 2026 to December 25, 2026 is 358 days (51 weeks and 1 days).

Adding days. 90 days after November 23, 2026 is February 21, 2027.

Counting Rules to Watch For

  • Whether the start date is counted. Contracts and deadlines often say "within 30 days after", which excludes the start day.
  • Month math: adding one month to January 31 gives the last day of February, since February is shorter.
  • Weekends and holidays are not skipped. For working days, subtract them separately.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it include the end date?

By default the difference counts the days between, not including the end day. Add one if you want an inclusive count.

How do I count business days?

Subtract weekends and holidays from the total days.

Does it handle leap years?

Yes.

What date is 90 days from today?

Enter today's date and add 90 days.

How many days are there in a year?

365 days, or 366 in a leap year. A leap year occurs every four years except most century years not divisible by 400.

How do I count business days between two dates?

Take the total days, remove weekends, then subtract any holidays that fall on weekdays.

Does the calculator include the end date?

Check the result label. Counting both ends adds one day compared with simple subtraction.
