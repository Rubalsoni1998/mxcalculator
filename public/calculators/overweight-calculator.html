About the Overweight Calculator

Being overweight means weighing more than the healthy range for your height. This calculator shows how far above (or below) that range your weight is, and the weight you would reach at a BMI of 25.

It is a simple way to turn a BMI number into a concrete weight-loss goal.

How to Use the Overweight Calculator

  1. Enter your height and current weight.
  2. Choose your units.
  3. Click Calculate to see your BMI, category and excess weight.

How Excess Weight Is Calculated

Weight at BMI 25 = 25 × height (m)². Excess weight is your current weight minus that number.

Example: A person who is 5 ft 10 in (70 in) and weighs 200 lb has a BMI of 703 × 200 ÷ 4,900 = 28.7. The weight at BMI 25 is 25 × 4,900 ÷ 703 = 174.3 lb, so the excess is about 25.7 lb.

Setting a Realistic Goal

Losing 5 to 10 percent of your body weight already lowers blood pressure, blood sugar and joint strain. Take it in steps instead of aiming for the full amount at once.

Worked Example: 5'9" and 200 lb

BMI = 703 × 200 ÷ 69² ≈ 29.5, in the overweight range (25 to 29.9). The top of the healthy range at this height (BMI 24.9) is about 169 lb, so the amount above it is about 31 lb.

Losing 5 to 10 percent of body weight, here 10 to 20 lb, is a common first goal, and is associated with health benefits for many people.

Setting Goals That Last

  • Aim for gradual change, often 1 to 2 pounds a week at most.
  • Pick habits you can keep: regular activity, sleep, and food you enjoy.
  • Use the calorie calculator to set a moderate deficit.

What This Number Cannot Say

Excess weight on a chart is not a diagnosis. Muscle mass, waist size and conditions such as high blood pressure or diabetes matter more for health decisions. Discuss goals with your doctor.

Frequently Asked Questions

At what BMI am I overweight?

A BMI from 25 to 29.9 is classed as overweight and 30 or more as obesity.

How fast can I safely lose weight?

About 0.5 to 1 kg (1 to 2 lb) per week is considered safe for most adults.

Should I aim for BMI 25 exactly?

It is a useful reference point, but even a modest loss brings health benefits.

Does this apply to muscular people?

Muscle adds weight, so BMI can overstate excess fat. Check body fat percentage too.

How many pounds overweight am I?

The calculator compares your weight with the top of the healthy BMI range for your height. The difference is the amount above it.

Is a BMI of 27 bad?

It falls in the overweight range. Health risk depends on other factors, so ask your doctor to interpret it with your waist size and test results.

How fast should I lose weight?

Slow, steady loss is generally easier to maintain. Many guidelines suggest around 1 to 2 pounds a week for adults, but individual advice should come from a clinician.
