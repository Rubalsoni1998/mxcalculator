About the Ideal Weight Calculator

Ideal body weight (IBW) formulas were created to estimate drug doses and later became popular for setting weight goals. Each formula uses height and gender to give a target in kilograms or pounds.

This calculator shows results from four well-known formulas side by side so you can see the range they suggest.

How to Use the Ideal Weight Calculator

  1. Select your gender.
  2. Enter your height and age.
  3. Click Calculate to see your ideal weight from each formula.

Ideal Weight Formulas

Devine: men 50 kg + 2.3 kg per inch over 5 ft; women 45.5 kg + 2.3 kg per inch over 5 ft.
Robinson: men 52 kg + 1.9 kg per inch; women 49 kg + 1.7 kg per inch.
Miller: men 56.2 kg + 1.41 kg per inch; women 53.1 kg + 1.36 kg per inch.
Hamwi: men 106 lb + 6 lb per inch; women 100 lb + 5 lb per inch (over 5 ft).

Example: Using Devine, a man who is 5 ft 10 in tall has an ideal weight of 50 + 2.3 × 10 = 73 kg (about 161 lb).

Use Ideal Weight Sensibly

These formulas ignore muscle, bone structure and body fat, so treat the result as a rough guide. Compare it with your healthy BMI range and body fat percentage.

Worked Example: Devine Formula

The Devine formula starts at 50 kg for men or 45.5 kg for women at 5 feet and adds 2.3 kg for each inch above that. A woman who is 5'6" gets 45.5 + 2.3 × 6 = 59.3 kg (131 lb). A man who is 5'10" gets 50 + 2.3 × 10 = 73.0 kg (161 lb).

Other formulas, such as Robinson, Miller and Hamwi, give somewhat different answers, which is why the calculator shows them side by side.

Where These Formulas Come From

Devine's formula was created in 1974 to help estimate medication doses, not to set weight goals. Several formulas have been in use since, each built on small samples. None accounts for muscle, bone structure or age.

A More Useful Way to Use the Result

  • Treat it as a reference point, not a target you must hit.
  • Compare it with the healthy BMI range on the healthy weight calculator.
  • Consider body composition with the body fat calculator.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which ideal weight formula is best?

None is perfect. Devine is most used in medicine; the others give similar results within a few kilograms.

Is ideal weight the same as healthy weight?

Not exactly. Ideal weight is a single number from a formula, while healthy weight is a range based on BMI.

Do the formulas work for short or tall people?

They are designed around heights above 5 ft and become less reliable at extremes.

Should I try to reach my ideal weight exactly?

Aim instead for a healthy range and good fitness. Small differences from the formula are not important.

Which ideal weight formula is most accurate?

None is clearly the best. They all give an estimate based on height and sex. Use the spread across formulas as a range and rely on health measures too.

Does ideal body weight include muscle?

Formulas ignore body composition, so an athletic person can weigh more than the result and still be healthy.

Why do the formulas give different results?

They were created from different groups of people and for different purposes, so their assumptions about height and weight differ.
