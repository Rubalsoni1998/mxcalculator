About the Hours Calculator

Whether you are an employee tracking a timesheet or a manager preparing payroll, calculating hours worked by hand is error-prone. This hours calculator totals your time per day and per week.

Enter start and end times, subtract unpaid breaks and see total hours in both hours and minutes and decimal format.

How to Use the Hours Calculator

  1. Enter the start time, end time and break for each day.
  2. Add as many rows as you need.
  3. Click Calculate to see daily and weekly totals.

How Hours Are Calculated

Hours worked = end time − start time − break. To convert minutes into a decimal, divide by 60 (for example 45 minutes = 0.75 hours).

Example: 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM with a 30-minute break is 8 hours of work.

Worked Example: A Shift With a Break

A shift from 9:15 AM to 5:45 PM is 8 hours 30 minutes. Subtract a 30-minute unpaid break and you get 8 hours. A shift from 8:00 AM to 4:50 PM with a 30-minute break is 8 hours 20 minutes, which is 8.33 decimal hours (20 ÷ 60 = 0.33). Five such days add up to 41.67 hours.

Converting Minutes to Decimals

Payroll often uses decimals: 15 minutes = 0.25, 30 minutes = 0.50, 45 minutes = 0.75. Divide minutes by 60 for any other value.

Overtime Basics

Under the federal Fair Labor Standards Act, most hourly employees are owed at least 1.5 times their regular rate for hours over 40 in a workweek. Some states have stricter daily rules. Confirm your own rules with the Department of Labor or your state agency. Turn hours into pay with the salary calculator, and add or subtract clock times with the time calculator.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert minutes to decimal hours?

Divide the minutes by 60.

How do I calculate overtime?

Hours beyond the weekly or daily limit set by law or contract are overtime.

Can I include shifts that pass midnight?

Yes, set the end time on the next day.

Does it work for payroll?

It totals hours; multiply by the hourly rate to find pay.

How do I calculate hours worked?

Subtract the start time from the end time, then subtract unpaid breaks. Convert minutes to decimals if you need payroll hours.

How do I convert 7 hours 45 minutes to decimal?

45 ÷ 60 = 0.75, so 7 hours 45 minutes is 7.75 hours.

When is overtime owed in the US?

Federal law requires overtime for covered, non-exempt employees after 40 hours in a workweek. Some states add daily limits.
