About the Triangle Calculator

A triangle has three sides and three angles that add up to 180 degrees. If you know enough of them, you can find the rest. This triangle calculator solves for sides, angles, area, perimeter and more.

It supports right triangles and general triangles.

How to Use the Triangle Calculator

  1. Enter any three known values, such as three sides or two sides and an angle.
  2. Click Calculate.
  3. Read the missing sides, angles, area and perimeter.

Key Triangle Formulas

Pythagorean theorem: a² + b² = c². Area (base and height): ½ × b × h. Heron: √(s(s−a)(s−b)(s−c)) with s the half-perimeter. Law of cosines: c² = a² + b² − 2ab cos C.

Example: A triangle with sides 3, 4 and 5 has area 6, perimeter 12 and angles of 36.87°, 53.13° and 90°.

Worked Examples

Law of cosines. Two sides of 7 and 9 with a 60° angle between them: c² = 7² + 9² − 2 × 7 × 9 × cos 60° = 67, so the third side is 8.185.

Heron's formula. For sides 5, 6 and 7, s = 9 and the area is √(9 × 4 × 3 × 2) = 14.70 square units.

Right triangle. Legs of 3 and 4 give a hypotenuse of 5, an area of 6, and a perimeter of 12.

Which Rule to Use

  • Right triangle with two sides known: Pythagorean theorem.
  • Two angles and a side (AAS or ASA): law of sines.
  • Three sides (SSS) or two sides and the angle between them (SAS): law of cosines.
  • Two sides and a non-included angle (SSA) can have 0, 1 or 2 solutions.

Check Your Inputs

The sum of any two sides must exceed the third, and angles must add to 180°. See the scientific calculator for trig functions and the concrete calculator for a practical use of area and volume.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find the area of a triangle?

Half the base times the height, or Heron's formula when you only know the sides.

How do I know if a triangle is right-angled?

If a² + b² = c².

Can any three lengths form a triangle?

No. The two shorter sides must add to more than the longest.

What is the sum of the angles?

180 degrees.

How do I find the area of a triangle with three sides?

Use Heron's formula: with s = (a + b + c) ÷ 2, area = √(s(s − a)(s − b)(s − c)).

What is the Pythagorean theorem?

In a right triangle, a² + b² = c², where c is the hypotenuse.

Why does my triangle have no solution?

The inputs may violate the triangle inequality or the angle sum, so no triangle can match them.
