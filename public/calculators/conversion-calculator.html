Common Conversion Factors

1 inch = 2.54 cm; 1 mile = 1.609 km; 1 kg = 2.2046 lb; 1 US gallon = 3.785 L; °F = °C × 9/5 + 32.

Example: 5 miles is 8.047 kilometres, and 100 °F equals 37.8 °C.

Temperature

°F = °C × 9/5 + 32. So 22.2°C = 72°F, and 0°C = 32°F. To go the other way, °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9: 72°F = 22.2°C.

Watch for US vs. Imperial Units

  • The US gallon (3.785 L) is smaller than the imperial gallon (4.546 L).
  • US fluid ounces differ slightly from imperial ones.
  • The "ton" can mean short ton (2,000 lb, US), long ton or metric tonne.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert inches to centimetres?

Multiply inches by 2.54.

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply by 9/5 and add 32.

Are US and UK gallons the same?

No. A US gallon is 3.785 litres and an imperial gallon is 4.546 litres.

Is this accurate?

Yes, it uses standard international conversion factors.

How many centimeters are in an inch?

Exactly 2.54 centimeters.

How do I convert pounds to kilograms?

Multiply pounds by 0.45359237. For example, 150 lb is about 68.04 kg.

Why are US and UK gallons different?

They come from different historical definitions: the US gallon is based on the old wine gallon, and the imperial gallon on the volume of 10 pounds of water.
