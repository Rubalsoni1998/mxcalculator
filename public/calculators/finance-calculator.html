About the Finance Calculator

The time value of money says that money today is worth more than the same amount later, because it can earn interest. This finance calculator works like a standard financial calculator: fill in four of the five values and solve for the missing one.

It handles loans, annuities, savings plans and leases.

How to Use the Finance Calculator

  1. Choose which value to solve for: N, I/Y, PV, PMT or FV.
  2. Fill in the other values, plus payments and compounding per year.
  3. Select the payment mode (end or beginning) and click Calculate.

TVM Relationship

PV + PMT × (1 − (1 + i)⁻ⁿ) ÷ i + FV × (1 + i)⁻ⁿ = 0, where i is the rate per period. Cash you pay out is entered as negative, and cash you receive as positive.

Example: Investing $10,000 (PV) at 5 percent for 10 years with no payments gives a future value of $16,288.95.

Worked Examples

Future value. Saving $200 a month for 10 years at 6% compounded monthly builds to about $32,776, of which $24,000 is your own deposits.

Present value. To have $50,000 in 8 years with a 5% annual return and no deposits, you would need about $33,842 today.

Payment. To repay $25,000 at 7% over 60 months, the payment is about $495.03 a month.

Sign Conventions

Time value of money problems track the direction of cash. Money you receive is positive and money you pay out is negative. If the result looks like it has the wrong sign, check whether the present value and the payment point the same way.

Choosing What to Solve For

  • Solve for payment when planning loans or savings targets.
  • Solve for rate to check what return or interest cost a deal implies.
  • Solve for periods to see how long a goal will take.

For specific cases, the loan calculator and investment calculator are simpler.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does PV mean?

Present value: the worth today of money in the future.

Why are some values negative?

Cash outflows are entered as negative and inflows as positive.

What is the difference between end and begin mode?

End mode assumes payments at the end of each period (ordinary annuity); begin mode at the start (annuity due).

Can I use it for a loan?

Yes. Enter loan amount as PV, rate, number of periods and solve for payment.

What does TVM mean?

TVM stands for time value of money: a dollar today is worth more than a dollar later because it can earn a return. The five linked values are present value, future value, payment, rate and number of periods.

What is the difference between present value and future value?

Present value is what a future amount is worth today. Future value is what a present amount will grow to after earning a return for a set time.

Why is the payment negative?

Finance calculators use cash-flow signs. A payment you make is an outflow and shows as negative, while a loan you receive is an inflow.
