About the Conception Calculator

If you know when conception happened, or when you got a positive test and counted back, you can estimate the rest of your pregnancy timeline. Pregnancy is counted from the last period, so conception happens about two weeks into it.

This calculator links your conception date, last period, due date and weeks pregnant in one place.

How to Use the Conception Calculator

  1. Choose what you know: conception date, due date or weeks pregnant.
  2. Enter the date or week.
  3. Click Calculate to see the matching dates.

How the Dates Are Linked

Due date = conception date + 266 days, and last period ≈ conception − 14 days. A positive home test usually appears about two weeks after conception.

Example: If conception happened on January 24, the due date is 266 days later, October 17, and your last period was about January 10.

Worked Example: From a Conception Date

If conception happened on January 24, 2026, the estimated due date is 266 days later: October 17, 2026. That is the same date as counting 280 days from an LMP of January 10, 2026 in a 28-day cycle.

Gestational Age vs. Fetal Age

Providers state pregnancy length as gestational age, counted from the LMP, so a pregnancy is already about two weeks along at conception. The baby's actual (fetal) age is about two weeks younger. This is why your doctor says "8 weeks" when conception was about 6 weeks ago.

When Dates Do Not Match

  • If you know your conception date from IVF or ovulation tracking, it can be more accurate than the LMP.
  • Large differences between methods are usually settled by an ultrasound.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find my conception date?

Count from the day you ovulated or had intercourse during your fertile window, or use your due date.

Is the result exact?

No. It is accurate to within a few days.

What is the difference between fetal age and gestational age?

Fetal age begins at conception; gestational age begins about two weeks earlier, at your last period.

How is the due date set for IVF?

From the egg retrieval or embryo transfer date, adjusted for embryo age.

How do I calculate a due date from conception?

Add 266 days (38 weeks) to the conception date.

Why is my pregnancy "two weeks" longer than the conception date suggests?

Gestational age starts at the last period, about two weeks before conception.

Is conception date the same as the day of intercourse?

Not always. Sperm can survive for days, so conception may occur several days after intercourse.
