About the Random Number Generator

A random number generator produces numbers with no predictable pattern. It is used for games, raffles, sampling, giveaways, teaching probability and picking lottery numbers.

This generator uses your browser's cryptographically secure random source, so every value in the range is equally likely.

How to Use the Random Number Generator

  1. Enter the minimum and maximum numbers.
  2. Choose how many numbers to generate and whether repeats are allowed.
  3. Click Generate to see your numbers.

How It Works

Each number is drawn uniformly from your range. With No repeats turned on, numbers are drawn without replacement, like pulling tickets from a hat.

Example: Choosing a range of 1 to 49 with six numbers and no repeats creates a set like 7, 12, 19, 23, 38, 41 for a lottery-style pick.

How the Numbers Are Produced

Each draw takes one 32-bit value from crypto.getRandomValues and scales it into your range. For whole numbers, the result is the minimum plus a random offset, and both ends of the range are included. A range of 1 to 6 can return 1 and 6. The scaling leaves an unevenness smaller than the range divided by about 4.3 billion, which is invisible for any range you would normally use.

The decimal option returns values rounded to two decimal places. If you enter a decimal minimum or maximum while whole numbers are selected, the tool rounds the minimum up and the maximum down. A count can be anywhere from 1 to 1,000, and with no repeats you cannot ask for more numbers than the range contains.

Worked Examples

Picking a winner. Number your entries 1 to N in a spreadsheet, then generate one number in that range. If 250 people entered, use 1 to 250 and count 1.

Rolling dice. Set 1 to 6 and generate two numbers with repeats allowed. There are 36 possible ordered results, and each has the same 1 in 36 chance, including a double six.

Lottery-style picks. A 6-of-49 game has 13,983,816 possible combinations. Choosing 1 to 49 with six numbers and no repeats gives one of them. Every combination, including 1-2-3-4-5-6, is equally likely, so a pattern is no better or worse than a scattered pick. The tool cannot raise your odds. Repeats, Sampling and Fairness

Allowing repeats is drawing with replacement, like rolling a die again. Turning repeats off is drawing without replacement, like pulling raffle tickets from a hat. Choose with replacement for independent events, and without replacement when each item can be chosen only once, such as selecting survey respondents from a numbered list.

True randomness has streaks. Seeing 4 three times in a row does not mean the next number is "due" to change. Each draw is independent. If you want to describe how spread out a batch of results is, the standard deviation calculator can help.

When Not to Rely on It

  • Official drawings. Sweepstakes, raffles and promotions are regulated in many states, and the rules can cover registration, odds disclosure and who may run a drawing. Check state and platform rules. A fair generator does not make a promotion legal.
  • Passwords and keys. The randomness source is strong, but numbers shown on screen are not a secure way to create credentials. Use the password generator.
  • Repeatable results. There is no seed option, so you cannot reproduce a run. If you need an auditable draw, record the numbers when you generate them.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the number really random?

It uses the browser's secure random source, which is suitable for most uses.

Can I generate numbers without repeats?

Yes. Turn on No repeats.

How do I simulate a dice roll?

Use a range of 1 to 6.

Can I use it for official drawings?

Check the rules of your contest. Many require an independently audited process.

Is this random number generator truly random?

It uses your browser's cryptographically secure random source, which is suitable for games, drawings and sampling. Strictly, it is a high-quality pseudorandom generator seeded from unpredictable system data, not a physical random process.

How do I pick a random winner from a list?

Number each entry from 1 to N, set the minimum to 1 and the maximum to N, and generate one number. For several winners, ask for that many numbers with no repeats so nobody wins twice.

What does no repeats mean in a random number generator?

No repeats means each number can appear only once in a batch, like drawing tickets without putting them back. It requires the range to hold at least as many different whole numbers as you request.

Can the same number come up twice in a row?

Yes, when repeats are allowed. Each draw is independent, so a repeat is as likely as any other specific value. With 1 to 10, the chance the next number matches the previous one is 1 in 10.
