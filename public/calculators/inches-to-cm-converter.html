About the Inches to Centimeters Converter

This converter changes inches to centimeters and back, and it also handles feet and inches together, millimeters, meters and yards. Height, screen sizes, clothing, lumber and crafts all mix inches and centimeters, so a fast and exact conversion saves guesswork. Heights in feet and inches are the most common request, so there is a dedicated feet and inches mode.

Conversion Formulas

cm = inches × 2.54
inches = cm ÷ 2.54
cm = (feet × 12 + inches) × 2.54
1 foot = 30.48 cm, 1 yard = 91.44 cm, 1 meter = 39.3701 in

Example: A person who is 5 feet 10 inches tall is (5 × 12 + 10) = 70 inches. Multiplied by 2.54 that is 177.8 cm. In the other direction, 180 cm ÷ 2.54 = 70.87 inches, which is 5 ft 10.87 in.

The Exact Definition

Under the 1959 international yard and pound agreement, one inch is defined as exactly 2.54 centimeters. Because of that definition the conversion is exact and does not need rounding, and a foot is exactly 30.48 centimeters, a yard exactly 0.9144 meters and a mile exactly 1.609344 kilometers.

Key Terms

  • Inch (in): A US customary unit of length, one twelfth of a foot.
  • Centimeter (cm): One hundredth of a meter, about the width of a fingernail.
  • Foot (ft): 12 inches, or exactly 30.48 centimeters.
  • Millimeter (mm): One tenth of a centimeter. Many tools, bolts and lumber sizes use millimeters.

Handy Mental Math

  • Inches to centimeters: multiply by 2.5 and add a little. 10 inches is about 25 cm (exact: 25.4).
  • Centimeters to inches: multiply by 0.4. 50 cm is about 20 inches (exact: 19.7).
  • Quick reference: 1 inch is about the width of an adult thumb joint, and 30 cm is just under a foot.
  • Screens: a 65 inch TV measures 65 × 2.54 = 165.1 cm across the diagonal.
