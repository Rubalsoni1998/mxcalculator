About the Countdown Calculator

This calculator counts the days, weeks, hours, minutes and seconds until any date you pick, or until the next date of a US holiday. It also counts weekdays, so you can plan work days before a deadline, and it works for dates in the past by showing how long ago the event was. While the page stays open the countdown ticks every second.

How the Calculation Works

Days = whole calendar days between today and the target date
Weeks and days = Days ÷ 7, with the remainder in days
Hours, minutes, seconds = exact time between now and the target moment
Weekdays = days that fall on Monday to Friday (weekends excluded)

Example: If today is November 25 and the target is December 25, there are 30 days, which is 4 weeks and 2 days. Counting only Monday to Friday, about 22 of those days are weekdays.

How the Holiday Dates Are Found

Many US holidays fall on a set weekday instead of a fixed date, so the calculator works them out for each year. The federal rules are: Martin Luther King Jr. Day is the third Monday in January, Presidents' Day is the third Monday in February, Memorial Day is the last Monday in May, Labor Day is the first Monday in September, Columbus Day is the second Monday in October and Thanksgiving is the fourth Thursday in November. Fixed-date holidays are New Year's Day (January 1), Juneteenth (June 19), Independence Day (July 4), Veterans Day (November 11) and Christmas (December 25). Mother's Day is the second Sunday in May and Father's Day the third Sunday in June. Easter falls on the first Sunday after the first full moon on or after March 21 and is calculated with the Gregorian computus.

Key Terms

  • Calendar days: Every day counted, including weekends and holidays.
  • Weekdays: Monday through Friday. A weekday count is not the same as business days, because holidays are not removed.
  • Local time: The countdown uses your device's time zone and clock.
  • Computus: The calculation that finds the date of Easter.

Tips for Using a Countdown

  • Set the time for deadlines. A noon deadline gives a different hour count than midnight.
  • Use weekdays for work plans. A project due in 30 days may only have about 22 work days.
  • Name your event. It makes the results easier to share or screenshot.
  • Mind time zones. If the event is in another time zone, convert the start time to your own first.
  • Check official dates. Confirm important dates such as tax deadlines with the official source.

The date and time come from your device. Daylight saving changes are handled by your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days until Christmas?

Pick Christmas Day in the quick pick menu to see the exact number of days, weeks, hours and minutes left from today.

How do I count down to a date?

Choose a quick pick holiday or select Custom date, enter the date and optionally the time, and the calculator shows the time remaining.

Does the countdown include weekends?

The main day count includes every calendar day. A separate line shows how many of those days are weekdays from Monday to Friday.

Can I count days since a past date?

Yes. Enter a date that has already passed and the calculator shows how long ago it was.

When is Thanksgiving?

Thanksgiving is the fourth Thursday in November. Use the quick pick to see the date for the coming year.

How is Easter calculated?

Easter is the first Sunday after the first full moon on or after March 21. The calculator uses the Gregorian computus to find the date.

Does the countdown update by itself?

Yes. While the page is open, the days, hours, minutes and seconds update every second.

Are federal holidays removed from the weekday count?

No. The weekday count only removes Saturdays and Sundays. Holidays are not subtracted, so the number of actual working days may be lower.
