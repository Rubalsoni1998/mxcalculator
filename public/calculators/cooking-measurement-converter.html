About the Cooking Measurement Converter

American recipes use cups, tablespoons and ounces, while most of the world cooks by weight in grams and by volume in milliliters. This converter handles both: it changes between teaspoons, tablespoons, fluid ounces, cups, pints, quarts, gallons, milliliters and liters, and it converts cups to grams and ounces for common baking ingredients.

Why Cups to Grams Varies

A cup is a measure of volume, and a gram is a measure of weight, so the answer depends on the ingredient. A cup of sugar weighs far more than a cup of flour. The figures here use values from King Arthur Baking's ingredient weight chart for flour, sugar and butter, and the standard densities of water, milk, oil and honey for the liquids. Sources disagree by a few grams, because how you fill the cup matters. Scooping flour with the cup can pack in 15% or more than spooning and leveling.

Key Terms

  • Fluid ounce: A unit of volume. A US fluid ounce is about 29.57 mL. It is not the same as an ounce of weight.
  • Stick of butter: A US stick is 1/2 cup, 8 tablespoons or 4 ounces (113 g).
  • Spoon and level: Spoon flour into the cup and level it off. This gives about 120 grams per cup.
  • Dip and sweep: Scooping the cup into the flour. It packs more in and can add 15% or more.

Cooking Conversion Tips

  • Weigh baking ingredients. A kitchen scale is the most reliable way to get consistent cakes and bread.
  • Check the cup size. A US cup is 236.6 mL, while a metric cup used in Australia and elsewhere is 250 mL.
  • Tablespoons differ. A US tablespoon is about 14.8 mL, but Australian recipes use 20 mL.
  • Liquids by volume, dry by weight. Use measuring cups for liquids and a scale for flour, sugar and cocoa.
  • Round sensibly. 1 cup is close enough to 240 mL for most savory cooking.

Ingredient weights are typical values. Brands, humidity and how you fill the cup can change them by several percent.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many tablespoons are in a cup?

There are 16 tablespoons in a US cup.

How many teaspoons are in a tablespoon?

There are 3 teaspoons in a tablespoon.

How many ounces are in a cup?

A US cup is 8 fluid ounces. In weight, a cup of water is about 8.3 ounces, while a cup of flour weighs about 4.25 ounces.

How many grams are in a cup of flour?

About 120 grams for all-purpose flour when you spoon it into the cup and level it, according to King Arthur Baking. Scooping can give 140 grams or more.

How many grams are in a cup of sugar?

About 198 grams for granulated sugar and about 213 grams for packed brown sugar, using King Arthur Baking figures.

How many cups is 1 stick of butter?

A US stick of butter is 1/2 cup, which is 8 tablespoons, 4 ounces or 113 grams.

How many milliliters are in a cup?

A US cup is 236.588 mL. Many recipes round it to 240 mL.

Why can I not convert cups to grams without picking an ingredient?

A cup measures volume and grams measure weight, so the answer depends on how dense the ingredient is. Choose an ingredient to include weights.
