About the Pace Calculator

Pace is how long it takes you to cover a set distance, usually shown as minutes per mile or minutes per kilometre. Knowing your pace helps you plan training runs, set race goals and avoid starting too fast.

This pace calculator solves for any one of pace, time or distance when you enter the other two, and shows equivalent speeds in miles and kilometres.

How to Use the Pace Calculator

  1. Choose what you want to find: pace, time or distance.
  2. Enter the other two values, or pick a standard race distance.
  3. Click Calculate to see the result with split times.

Pace Formulas

Pace = time ÷ distance, Time = pace × distance and Speed = distance ÷ time. To convert, 1 mile = 1.609 km, so pace per mile = pace per km × 1.609.

Example: Running a 5K (3.1 miles) in 25:00 is a pace of 5:00 per km, or about 8:03 per mile. A 4-hour marathon needs roughly 5:41 per km (9:09 per mile).

Common Race Distances

  • 5K: 3.11 miles
  • 10K: 6.21 miles
  • Half marathon: 13.1 miles (21.1 km)
  • Marathon: 26.2 miles (42.2 km)

Worked Examples

10K at 8:30 per mile. A 10K is 6.214 miles, so the finish time is about 52:49 (minutes:seconds).

Marathon in 4 hours. The marathon is 26.2 miles, so you need about 9:09 per mile.

Pacing Tips

  • Run the first mile slightly slower than goal pace; going out too fast is the most common mistake.
  • For races longer than a 10K, steady effort beats alternating fast and slow miles.
  • Hills and heat slow pace; adjust expectations on tough courses.

Check effort with heart rate zones, and use the unit converter for miles and kilometers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good running pace?

It depends on your fitness. Many recreational runners run between 9:00 and 12:00 minutes per mile.

How do I convert min/mile to min/km?

Divide the minutes per mile by 1.609.

How can I improve my pace?

Mix easy runs with intervals and tempo runs, build mileage gradually and get enough rest.

What pace should I run a marathon?

Divide your target finish time by 26.2 miles for an even pace, and start slightly slower.

How do I convert pace per mile to pace per kilometer?

Divide minutes per mile by 1.609. For example, 8:00 per mile is about 4:58 per kilometer.

What is a good 5K time?

It depends on age, experience and goals. Finishing comfortably is a good first target; use the calculator to set a realistic pace.

How many miles is a half marathon?

A half marathon is 13.1 miles, or 21.1 kilometers.
