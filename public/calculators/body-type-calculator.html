About the Body Type Calculator

Your body shape describes how weight and proportions are distributed across your shoulders, bust, waist and hips. Knowing it helps with choosing clothing cuts, and the waist-to-hip ratio linked to it is also a simple health indicator.

Enter your measurements and the calculator identifies your shape and gives your waist-to-hip ratio.

How to Use the Body Type Calculator

  1. Measure your bust or chest, waist and hips with a soft tape.
  2. Enter the values in the form.
  3. Click Calculate to see your body shape and ratio.

How Body Type Is Determined

Waist-to-hip ratio = waist ÷ hips. The shape comes from comparing bust, waist and hip sizes: similar bust and hips with a clearly smaller waist suggest an hourglass, wider hips suggest a pear, a larger waist suggests an apple, and similar measurements throughout suggest a rectangle.

Example: A waist of 72 cm and hips of 100 cm gives a ratio of 0.72, which is a pear or hourglass proportion depending on the bust measurement.

Waist-to-Hip Ratio and Health

Carrying more weight around the waist is associated with a higher risk of heart disease and type 2 diabetes. The World Health Organization considers a ratio above about 0.85 for women and 0.90 for men as a sign of increased risk.

Worked Example: Waist-to-Hip Ratio

Waist-to-hip ratio is waist circumference divided by hip circumference. A 32-inch waist with 40-inch hips gives 0.80. A 38-inch waist with 40-inch hips gives 0.95. The World Health Organization lists a ratio of 0.90 or higher for men and 0.85 or higher for women as a marker of abdominal obesity.

How to Measure

  • Measure waist at the narrowest point, or at the midpoint between the lowest rib and the top of the hip bone.
  • Measure hips at the widest part of the buttocks.
  • Stand relaxed, with the tape level and snug.

What Body Shape Tells You

Shape labels like "apple" or "pear" describe where weight tends to be carried. They are not diagnoses. Fat around the abdomen is linked with higher health risk than fat on the hips and thighs, which is why waist measures are useful alongside BMI. See the BMI calculator and body fat calculator.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I measure my waist correctly?

Measure at the narrowest point of your torso, or at the navel if there is no clear narrowing, after breathing out normally.

Can my body type change?

Weight changes, pregnancy, muscle growth and age can alter your proportions, though bone structure stays the same.

What body type is healthiest?

Shape itself is not a measure of health. What matters is overall fitness, and a lower waist-to-hip ratio is generally linked with lower risk.

Is this the same as somatotype?

No. Somatotypes such as ectomorph or mesomorph describe frame and muscularity. This tool focuses on shape from proportions.

What is a healthy waist-to-hip ratio?

The WHO cites below 0.90 for men and below 0.85 for women as lower-risk. Individual risk depends on other factors too.

Can I change my body shape?

Genetics largely set where fat is stored, but overall fat loss and strength training can change measurements.

Is WHR better than BMI?

They measure different things. WHR reflects fat distribution, while BMI compares weight with height. Using both gives a fuller picture.
