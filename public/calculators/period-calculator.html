About the Period Calculator

A menstrual cycle is counted from the first day of one period to the first day of the next. The average is 28 days, but anything from 21 to 35 days is considered normal in adults.

This period calculator uses your last period date, cycle length and period length to predict your next periods and show the dates on a calendar.

How to Use the Period Calculator

  1. Enter the first day of your last period.
  2. Enter your average cycle length and period length.
  3. Click Calculate to see your next period and the following cycles.

How Predictions Work

Next period = last period start + cycle length. The result repeats for later cycles. Predictions are most accurate if your cycle is regular.

Example: If your last period began May 3 and your cycle is 28 days, your next period is expected on May 31.

When to See a Doctor

  • Periods stop for three months or more (and you are not pregnant)
  • Cycles shorter than 21 days or longer than 35 days
  • Very heavy bleeding or severe pain

Worked Example

If your last period started January 10, 2026 and your cycle averages 28 days, the next periods are predicted for February 7, 2026, March 7, 2026, April 4, 2026. Cycle length is counted from the first day of one period to the first day of the next.

What Counts as Normal

ACOG describes cycles of 21 to 35 days in adults as normal, with bleeding that lasts around 2 to 7 days. Cycles can vary by a few days each month, which is why the calculator gives an estimate rather than a promise.

Improving Predictions

  • Record start dates for several months and use your average.
  • Stress, travel, illness, weight change and hormonal methods can shift the date.
  • If you are late and could be pregnant, take a pregnancy test and see the pregnancy calculator.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is a normal period?

Two to seven days is common.

Why is my period late?

Stress, travel, illness, weight change, hormones and pregnancy can all delay it.

Is my cycle regular?

If it varies by fewer than about seven days from month to month, it is generally considered regular.

How do I track my cycle?

Note the first day of bleeding each month in a calendar or app.

How many days is a normal menstrual cycle?

Adults commonly have cycles of 21 to 35 days. Teens may have slightly longer ones.

Why is my period late?

Common reasons are stress, illness, weight changes, hormonal changes or pregnancy. See a clinician if it happens often or you have other symptoms.

When should I see a doctor about my period?

Talk to a clinician if cycles are consistently shorter than 21 or longer than 35 days, bleeding is very heavy, or periods stop for 3 months or more.
