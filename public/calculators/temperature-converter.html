About the Temperature Converter

This converter changes a temperature between Fahrenheit, Celsius, Kelvin and Rankine in one step. The United States uses Fahrenheit for weather, cooking and body temperature, while most of the world and nearly all of science use Celsius or Kelvin, so a quick conversion comes up often for travel, recipes and homework.

Conversion Formulas

°C = (°F − 32) × 5 ÷ 9
°F = °C × 9 ÷ 5 + 32
K = °C + 273.15
°R = °F + 459.67

Example: Convert 72°F to Celsius: (72 − 32) × 5 ÷ 9 = 40 × 5 ÷ 9 = 22.2°C. Convert 20°C to Fahrenheit: 20 × 9 ÷ 5 + 32 = 68°F.

The Celsius and Fahrenheit scales meet at −40, where −40°F equals −40°C. Water boils at 100°C only at standard sea-level pressure, and the boiling point drops as altitude increases. Normal body temperature varies from person to person and through the day, so 98.6°F (37°C) is an average, not a rule.

Key Terms

  • Celsius: A scale where water freezes at 0° and boils at 100° at sea level.
  • Fahrenheit: A scale where water freezes at 32° and boils at 212° at sea level.
  • Kelvin: The SI base unit of temperature. It starts at absolute zero, so it has no negative values, and one kelvin is the same size as one degree Celsius.
  • Rankine: An absolute scale with Fahrenheit-sized degrees, used in some engineering fields.

Mental Math Shortcuts

  • Celsius to Fahrenheit, roughly: double it and add 30. For 20°C that gives 70°F, close to the exact 68°F.
  • Fahrenheit to Celsius, roughly: subtract 30 and halve it. For 70°F that gives 20°C.
  • Exact tip: every 10°C is exactly 18°F, so 10°C is 50°F, 20°C is 68°F and 30°C is 86°F.
  • Cooking: many recipes list both. When a recipe gives only °F, 350°F is about 177°C and most ovens treat it as 175°C or 180°C.

Results are rounded for display. Kelvin and Rankine cannot go below zero, so values below absolute zero are rejected.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert Fahrenheit to Celsius?

Subtract 32 from the Fahrenheit temperature, multiply by 5 and divide by 9. For example, 98.6°F is (98.6 − 32) × 5 ÷ 9 = 37°C.

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply the Celsius temperature by 9, divide by 5 and add 32. For example, 25°C is 25 × 9 ÷ 5 + 32 = 77°F.

What is 350°F in Celsius?

350°F is about 176.7°C, which most recipes round to 175°C or 180°C.

At what temperature are Fahrenheit and Celsius the same?

At −40. A temperature of −40°F equals −40°C.

What is room temperature in Fahrenheit and Celsius?

Room temperature is usually 68 to 72°F, which is about 20 to 22°C.

What is the boiling point of water in Fahrenheit?

212°F, or 100°C, at standard sea-level pressure. At higher altitudes water boils at a lower temperature.

What is the difference between Celsius and Kelvin?

They have the same size degree. Kelvin starts at absolute zero (0 K = −273.15°C), so you convert by adding 273.15 to the Celsius value.

What is a normal body temperature?

The long-quoted average is 98.6°F (37°C), but healthy people vary and temperatures change during the day. A reading of 100.4°F (38°C) or higher is commonly treated as a fever.
