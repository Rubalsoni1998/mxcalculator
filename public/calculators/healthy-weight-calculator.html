About the Healthy Weight Calculator

There is no single perfect weight. Doctors use a healthy weight range, which is the span of weights for your height that fall within the normal BMI category.

Enter your height and this calculator shows the lowest and highest weights in the healthy range and compares your current weight with it.

How to Use the Healthy Weight Calculator

  1. Enter your height and current weight.
  2. Choose your preferred units.
  3. Click Calculate to see your healthy weight range.

How the Range Is Calculated

Since BMI = weight ÷ height², the healthy range is found by rearranging: weight = BMI × height (m)² using BMI values 18.5 and 24.9.

Example: For a height of 170 cm (1.70 m): 18.5 × 2.89 = 53.5 kg and 24.9 × 2.89 = 72 kg. The healthy weight range is about 53.5 to 72 kg (118 to 159 lb).

Beyond the Scale

Frame size, muscle mass, age and body fat distribution all matter. If you are outside the range but feel well and have healthy blood pressure and blood sugar, speak with your doctor about what is right for you.

Worked Example: 5'6"

The weight range for a BMI of 18.5 to 24.9 is found with weight (lb) = BMI × height² (in) ÷ 703. At 66 inches, that is about 115 to 154 lb. Someone at 5'6" and 180 lb has a BMI near 29.0, above that range.

Why a Range, Not a Number

Healthy bodies come in many sizes. A range recognizes differences in frame, muscle mass and age. Where you sit within it matters less than other signs of health such as blood pressure, blood sugar, fitness and how you feel.

Limits of the BMI Range

  • BMI does not separate muscle from fat, so very muscular people can fall into "overweight" while being healthy.
  • Older adults may do better at the upper end of the range.
  • Ethnic background and body shape can change risk at a given BMI.
  • Waist size is a useful addition; see the body type calculator.

For your exact BMI, use the BMI calculator. Talk to your doctor about personal weight goals.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a healthy weight for my height?

It is the weight range corresponding to a BMI of 18.5 to 24.9 for your height.

Is being slightly above range unhealthy?

Not necessarily. Risk rises gradually, and fitness and waist size matter alongside weight.

Does the range change with age?

Adult ranges are the same, though some experts suggest slightly higher BMIs may be fine for older adults.

How is this different from ideal weight?

Ideal weight formulas give a single estimate, while the healthy range shows a span.

What is a healthy weight for my height?

It is the weight that gives a BMI between 18.5 and 24.9. Enter your height and the calculator converts that into pounds or kilograms.

Is a BMI of 25 unhealthy?

A BMI of 25 is the start of the overweight category, but it is only a screening measure. Waist size, fitness and lab results give a fuller view.

Does age change the healthy weight range?

The standard BMI range is the same for adults, but some doctors accept slightly higher values in older adults. Ask your clinician what is right for you.
