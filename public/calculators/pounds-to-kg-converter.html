About the Pounds to Kilograms Converter

This converter turns pounds into kilograms and back, and also handles ounces, grams and stone. It shows every result at once, including pounds and ounces together, which is how US scales and baby weights are usually read. Body weight, luggage, shipping and recipes all use these units, and the US is one of the few countries that still uses pounds for everyday weights.

Conversion Formulas

kg = lb × 0.45359237
lb = kg ÷ 0.45359237 (about kg × 2.20462)
oz = lb × 16 and g = oz × 28.349523125
1 stone = 14 pounds

Example: 150 lb × 0.45359237 = 68.04 kg. Going the other way, 70 kg ÷ 0.45359237 = 154.32 lb, or 154 lb 5.1 oz.

The Exact Definition

Since the 1959 international agreement among the US and other English-speaking countries, one avoirdupois pound is defined as exactly 0.45359237 kilograms. That means the pound is now a fixed fraction of the kilogram rather than a separate standard, and one ounce is exactly 28.349523125 grams. The commonly used shortcut of 2.2 pounds per kilogram is close but about 0.2% off, so use 2.20462 when accuracy matters.

Key Terms

  • Pound (lb): The US customary unit of mass, equal to 16 ounces.
  • Kilogram (kg): The SI base unit of mass, equal to 1,000 grams.
  • Stone (st): A British unit equal to 14 pounds, still used for body weight in the UK and Ireland.
  • Avoirdupois: The everyday system of weights in which a pound has 16 ounces. It is different from the troy ounce used for precious metals.

Handy Mental Math

  • Pounds to kilograms: halve the pounds and take off 10% of that. For 200 lb, half is 100, minus 10 is 90 kg (exact: 90.7).
  • Kilograms to pounds: double the kilograms and add 10%. For 50 kg, double is 100, plus 10 is 110 lb (exact: 110.2).
  • Airline bags: a 50 lb checked bag limit is about 22.7 kg, which is why many airlines list 23 kg.
  • Baby weights: use the pounds and ounces result to match what hospitals and pediatricians record.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert pounds to kilograms?

Multiply the number of pounds by 0.45359237. For example, 150 pounds is 150 × 0.45359237 = 68.04 kilograms.

How do I convert kilograms to pounds?

Multiply the kilograms by 2.20462 or divide by 0.45359237. For example, 70 kg is about 154.32 pounds.

How many pounds are in a kilogram?

One kilogram equals about 2.20462 pounds.

How many kilograms is 100 pounds?

100 pounds is 45.36 kilograms.

How many pounds is 1 kg of weight in pounds and ounces?

1 kg is 2.20462 pounds, which is 2 pounds and about 3.27 ounces.

What is a stone in pounds and kilograms?

A stone is 14 pounds, which is about 6.35 kilograms. It is mainly used in the UK and Ireland for body weight.

Is the pound exactly defined?

Yes. Since 1959 an avoirdupois pound is defined as exactly 0.45359237 kilograms.

Why do airlines use 23 kg?

A common checked bag limit of 50 pounds equals about 22.7 kilograms, so airlines that use metric units round to 23 kg.
