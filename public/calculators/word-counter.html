About the Word Counter

This tool counts words and characters as you type, then adds the details writers and students need: characters with and without spaces, sentences, paragraphs, average word length, reading and speaking time, and the words you use most. Your text stays in your browser and is never uploaded.

How the Counts Work

Words = groups of characters separated by spaces or line breaks
Characters = every character, including spaces (and a count without spaces)
Sentences = runs of text ending in . ! or ?
Paragraphs = blocks of text separated by a blank line
Reading time = Words ÷ Words per minute
Keyword density = Times a word appears ÷ Total words

Example: A 600-word article read silently at the average adult pace of 238 words per minute takes about 2.5 minutes (600 ÷ 238). Spoken at 130 words per minute, the same text runs about 4.6 minutes.

Reading and Speaking Speeds

A 2019 review of 190 studies found that adults read English non-fiction silently at an average of 238 words per minute and fiction at about 260. Reading aloud averages 183 words per minute. A prepared speech with natural pauses is slower, usually 130 to 150 words per minute. Faster rates reported in popular articles, such as 300 words per minute or more, are not typical for most people.

Key Terms

  • Character count: The total number of letters, numbers, punctuation marks and spaces. Many limits count spaces.
  • Keyword density: How often a word appears compared with the total word count. Natural writing matters more than hitting a target.
  • Words per minute (wpm): The rate used to estimate reading or speaking time.
  • Stop words: Very common words such as "the" and "and." They are left out of the keyword list.

Tips for Better Writing

  • Cut filler. Short, direct sentences are easier to read and score fewer words.
  • Vary sentence length. An average of 15 to 20 words per sentence reads comfortably for most audiences.
  • Check limits before you publish. Use the limit option to see how many characters are left.
  • Time your speech. Divide your word count by 130 to 150 to estimate a talk, and add time for pauses and slides.
  • Proofread. A count does not catch errors, so read your text one more time.

Counts are computed in your browser. Different programs may count hyphenated words, numbers and symbols a little differently.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I count words in a text?

Paste your text into the box and the word count updates instantly. Words are groups of characters separated by spaces or line breaks.

How is reading time calculated?

Divide the word count by the reading speed. Adults read English non-fiction silently at an average of about 238 words per minute, so a 1,000-word article takes about 4.2 minutes.

How long does it take to speak 500 words?

At a typical speech pace of 130 to 150 words per minute, 500 words takes roughly 3.3 to 3.8 minutes. At 183 words per minute, the pace for reading aloud, it takes about 2.7 minutes.

What is the character limit for a tweet?

A standard X (Twitter) post is limited to 280 characters. Premium accounts may have longer limits, so check the current rules.

Do characters include spaces?

This tool shows both counts. Many limits, such as the 280 character post limit, count spaces, while some forms ask for characters without spaces.

How many words is a page?

It depends on font, size and spacing. A common estimate is about 500 words for a single-spaced page and about 250 to 300 words for a double-spaced page.

Is my text saved or uploaded?

No. All counting happens in your browser and nothing is sent to a server.

What is a good keyword density?

There is no required number. Write naturally and use your main topic words where they fit. Stuffing keywords makes text harder to read and does not help.
