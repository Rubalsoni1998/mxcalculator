About the Age Calculator

Your age is the time between your date of birth and a chosen date. This calculator gives it in years, months and days, and also shows total months, weeks, days and the time until your next birthday.

It is useful for forms, school and job applications, legal age checks and just for fun.

How to Use the Age Calculator

  1. Enter your date of birth.
  2. Enter the date to calculate age on (today by default).
  3. Click Calculate to see your exact age.

How Age Is Calculated

Subtract the birth date from the target date, borrowing months and days as needed, taking each month's length and leap years into account.

Example: Someone born on March 15, 1990 is, on November 22, 2026, 36 years, 8 months and 7 days old.

Worked Example

Someone born on March 15, 1990 is, on November 22, 2026, 36 years, 8 months and 7 days old. That is 13,401 days in total. Age is counted as completed years, so you turn a year older on your birthday, not before.

Leap Day Birthdays

People born on February 29 have a birthday only in leap years. In non-leap years the legal birthday varies by state and context, often treated as February 28 or March 1.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate my age manually?

Subtract your birth year from the current year, then subtract one if your birthday has not happened yet this year.

Does it count leap years?

Yes. Leap days are included in day counts.

Can I find age on a past or future date?

Yes. Change the date to calculate on.

What is the age of someone born on February 29?

Their birthday is usually treated as February 28 or March 1 in non-leap years.

How do I calculate my age in days?

Subtract the birth date from today. The calculator counts every day, including leap days.

How old will I be on a future date?

Enter the future date in the comparison field to see your age on that day.

Is age counted differently in some countries?

Yes. Some countries use traditional systems that count a person as one at birth. The US counts completed years.
