About the Conception Calculator

Pregnancy is counted from the first day of your last period, but conception happens about two weeks later, around ovulation. If you know your due date or last period, you can estimate when conception probably occurred.

This conception calculator works backwards from your due date or forwards from your last period to give you a likely conception window.

How to Use the Conception Calculator

  1. Select whether you know your due date or last period date.
  2. Enter the date and your usual cycle length.
  3. Click Calculate to see your estimated conception date and fertile window.

How Conception Is Estimated

Conception date = due date − 266 days, or last period + (cycle length − 14) days. Sperm can survive up to five days, so conception can happen from a few days before to a day after ovulation.

Example: With a due date of October 17, conception is estimated at 266 days earlier, January 24, give or take a few days.

Why It Is a Window, Not a Day

Because sperm can live up to five days and an egg survives about 12 to 24 hours, the exact day of conception is rarely known. Treat the result as a range of about a week.

Worked Example: A 30-Day Cycle

Ovulation usually occurs about 14 days before the next period starts. With an LMP of January 10, 2026 and a 30-day cycle, ovulation falls around day 16, about January 26, 2026. The most likely conception window is the five days before ovulation through ovulation day, here January 21, 2026 to January 26, 2026.

Working Backward From a Due Date

If you know the due date, subtract 266 days (38 weeks) to estimate conception, since pregnancy counted from LMP is about 14 days longer than the time since conception. This gives a single day, but the real window is wider because sperm can survive up to five days in the reproductive tract and the egg lives about 12 to 24 hours.

Limits

  • The result is an estimate, especially with irregular cycles.
  • An early ultrasound is a more reliable way to date a pregnancy.
  • Calculating conception is not a way to establish paternity.

To convert in the other direction use the conception date to due date calculator; track fertility with the ovulation calculator.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is a conception date?

It is accurate to within several days. Cycle variation and the time sperm wait for the egg both affect it.

Does conception happen on the day of intercourse?

Not always. It can occur up to five days afterward.

How is gestational age different from fetal age?

Gestational age counts from your last period; fetal age counts from conception, about two weeks later.

Can ultrasound confirm the date?

Yes. An early scan measures the embryo and gives a precise estimate.

How many days after my period do I conceive?

In a typical 28-day cycle, conception happens around day 14 to 16 counted from the first day of the last period. This varies with cycle length.

Can I conceive on any day of my cycle?

Pregnancy is only possible during the fertile window, roughly five days before ovulation through the day after. Cycles vary, so timing can shift.

How does an ultrasound date conception?

It measures the embryo or fetus and compares it with growth standards. In early pregnancy this is usually accurate to within a few days.
