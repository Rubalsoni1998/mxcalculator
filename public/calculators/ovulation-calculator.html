About the Ovulation Calculator

Ovulation is when an ovary releases an egg. You are fertile for about six days each cycle: the five days before ovulation and the day of ovulation. Timing intercourse in that window gives the best chance of pregnancy.

This ovulation calculator predicts your fertile window and ovulation date from your last period and average cycle length.

How to Use the Ovulation Calculator

  1. Enter the first day of your last period.
  2. Enter your average cycle length in days.
  3. Click Calculate to see your ovulation day, fertile window and next period.

How Ovulation Is Predicted

Ovulation usually happens about 14 days before your next period: Ovulation day = cycle start + (cycle length − 14). The fertile window runs from five days before to one day after.

Example: With a 30-day cycle starting March 1, your next period is expected March 31 and ovulation about March 17, so the fertile window is March 12 to 17.

Signs of Ovulation

  • Clear, stretchy cervical mucus like raw egg white
  • A small rise in basal body temperature after ovulation
  • Mild one-sided pelvic pain
  • A positive result on an ovulation predictor (LH) test

Worked Example

With a 30-day cycle starting January 10, 2026, ovulation is expected around day 16, on about January 26, 2026, and the fertile window runs from January 21, 2026 to January 26, 2026. In a 28-day cycle, ovulation would come on day 14. Cycle length matters because ovulation is tied to the end of the cycle, not the start.

Combining a Calculator With Body Signals

  • Cervical mucus often becomes clear and slippery before ovulation.
  • Ovulation predictor kits detect the LH surge, usually 24 to 36 hours before ovulation.
  • Basal body temperature rises slightly after ovulation, so it confirms it afterward rather than predicting it.

When Predictions Are Less Reliable

Irregular cycles, recent hormonal contraception, breastfeeding, and conditions such as PCOS make calendar predictions less accurate. If you have been trying to conceive for 12 months (6 months if over 35), a clinician can help. See also the period calculator.

Frequently Asked Questions

When am I most fertile?

In the two days before ovulation and on the day itself.

Can I get pregnant outside the fertile window?

It is unlikely, but irregular cycles make predictions less certain.

What if my cycle is irregular?

Use LH tests or basal temperature tracking instead of calendar predictions.

How long does an egg survive?

About 12 to 24 hours after release.

How long does ovulation last?

The egg survives about 12 to 24 hours after release, but sperm can live up to five days, which is why the fertile window is about six days.

Can I get pregnant right after my period?

It is less likely, but possible with short cycles because sperm can survive several days before ovulation.

Are ovulation calculators accurate?

They estimate from your average cycle. Tracking signs or using predictor kits improves accuracy.
