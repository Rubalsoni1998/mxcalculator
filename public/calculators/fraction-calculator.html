About the Fraction Calculator

A fraction shows part of a whole as a numerator over a denominator. This fraction calculator performs the four basic operations on fractions and mixed numbers and returns the result in simplest form, as a mixed number and as a decimal.

It is helpful for homework, cooking, woodworking and any task involving parts of a whole.

How to Use the Fraction Calculator

  1. Enter the first fraction or mixed number.
  2. Select an operation: add, subtract, multiply or divide.
  3. Enter the second fraction and click Calculate.

Fraction Rules

Add/subtract: find a common denominator. Multiply: a/b × c/d = ac/bd. Divide: multiply by the reciprocal: a/b ÷ c/d = ad/bc. Simplify by dividing by the greatest common divisor.

Example: 1/2 + 1/3 = 3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6. And 3/4 ÷ 1/2 = 3/4 × 2/1 = 6/4 = 3/2 or 1 1/2.

Worked Examples

Adding. 3/4 + 5/6: use the common denominator 12. 9/12 + 10/12 = 19/12 = 1 7/12.

Dividing. 2/3 ÷ 4/9 = 2/3 × 9/4 = 18/12 = 3/2 (simplified).

To a decimal. 7/8 = 0.875.

Simplifying

Divide the numerator and denominator by their greatest common divisor. For 18/12 the GCD is 6, which gives 3/2. A fraction is fully simplified when the two numbers share no common factor other than 1.

Mixed Numbers

Convert a mixed number to an improper fraction before multiplying or dividing: 2 1/2 = 5/2. See the percentage calculator for percents and the scientific calculator for more.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add fractions with different denominators?

Convert them to a common denominator, add the numerators and simplify.

How do I simplify a fraction?

Divide the numerator and denominator by their greatest common divisor.

How do I convert a fraction to a decimal?

Divide the numerator by the denominator.

What is a mixed number?

A whole number combined with a fraction, such as 1 1/2.

How do I add fractions with different denominators?

Find a common denominator, convert each fraction, add the numerators, then simplify.

How do I divide fractions?

Multiply by the reciprocal of the second fraction: a/b ÷ c/d = a/b × d/c.

How do I turn a fraction into a percent?

Divide the numerator by the denominator and multiply by 100. For example, 3/4 = 0.75 = 75%.
