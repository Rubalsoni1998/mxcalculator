About the Grade Calculator

Most courses combine assignments, quizzes and exams with different weights. This grade calculator works out your current weighted grade and tells you what you need on the remaining work.

It is a quick way to plan your studying before the final.

How to Use the Grade Calculator

  1. Enter each assignment's score and weight.
  2. Enter your target grade and the weight of the final exam.
  3. Click Calculate to see your current grade and the score needed.

Grade Formulas

Weighted grade = Σ(score × weight) ÷ Σ weights. Needed final score: (target − current × (1 − final weight)) ÷ final weight.

Example: With an 85 percent current grade and a final worth 30 percent, a target of 88 requires (88 − 85 × 0.7) ÷ 0.3 = 95 percent on the final.

Worked Example: What Do I Need on the Final?

Suppose homework is 20% of your grade (you have 92), the midterm is 30% (you scored 78) and the final is worth 50%. Your points so far are 0.20 × 92 + 0.30 × 78 = 41.8. To finish with an 85, you need a final score of (85 − 41.8) ÷ 0.50 = 86.4%.

Checks Before You Rely on It

  • Make sure the weights add up to 100%.
  • Confirm whether your instructor drops the lowest score or rounds up.
  • If the needed score is above 100, the target is not reachable without extra credit.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a weighted grade?

Multiply each score by its weight, add the results and divide by the total weight.

What if the needed score is above 100?

Then the target is not achievable without extra credit.

Should weights add to 100?

They should, or the calculator divides by the total.

Can I drop the lowest score?

Remove it from the list before calculating.

How do I calculate a weighted grade?

Multiply each category score by its weight, then add them. Weights should total 100%.

What grade do I need on the final to get an A?

Enter your current grade, the final's weight and the grade you want; the calculator solves for the score needed.

Can I still pass if I failed the midterm?

Possibly, depending on the weights. Enter your scores to see what the remaining work must be.
