About the Pregnancy Calculator

The pregnancy calculator shows where you are in your pregnancy and what dates lie ahead. Pregnancy lasts about 40 weeks from the first day of your last period and is divided into three trimesters.

Enter a date and the calculator returns your due date, weeks and days pregnant, trimester and a timeline of milestones.

How to Use the Pregnancy Calculator

  1. Select the type of date you know.
  2. Enter the date and cycle length.
  3. Click Calculate to see your due date, week and trimester.

How Pregnancy Dates Are Counted

Gestational age is counted from the first day of the last period. Due date = last period + 280 days. If you know the conception date, add 266 days instead.

Example: If your last period began January 10, on April 4 you are 12 weeks and 0 days pregnant.

Key Milestones

  • Week 8–12: first prenatal visit and heartbeat scan
  • Week 11–14: first-trimester screening
  • Week 18–22: anatomy ultrasound
  • Week 24–28: glucose screening
  • Week 36 onward: weekly checkups

Worked Example: Where Are You Today?

If your LMP was January 10, 2026, you reach week 12 on April 4, 2026, the usual start of the second trimester is around week 13 (April 11, 2026), and the third trimester starts around week 28 (July 25, 2026). The due date is October 17, 2026.

Trimesters at a Glance

  • First trimester: weeks 1 to 12, when major organs form.
  • Second trimester: weeks 13 to 27, often when movement is first felt.
  • Third trimester: weeks 28 to birth.

Using the Dates

Use these dates to plan prenatal visits and screening, which providers schedule by gestational week. Weight guidance is on the pregnancy weight gain calculator, and dating details are on the due date calculator page. This tool does not replace medical care.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many weeks is a full pregnancy?

About 40 weeks from the last period, or 38 weeks from conception.

How is gestational age measured?

From the first day of your last period, which is about two weeks before conception.

When does the second trimester start?

At week 14.

Can the due date change?

Yes. An early ultrasound can adjust it by several days.

How many weeks is a full pregnancy?

Pregnancy is counted as about 40 weeks from the first day of the last period.

When does the second trimester start?

Usually around week 13, although some sources count from week 14.

How far along am I?

Count weeks from the first day of your last period, or from conception date plus two weeks. The calculator does this for you.
