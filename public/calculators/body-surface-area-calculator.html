About the Body Surface Area Calculator

Body surface area (BSA) is the measured or calculated surface area of the human body, usually shown in square metres. Clinicians use it to scale doses of some medicines such as chemotherapy, to size cardiac output, and to compare kidney function across body sizes.

Enter your height and weight and the calculator returns BSA using the Mosteller formula along with other accepted formulas, so you can compare the results.

BSA Formulas

Mosteller: BSA (m²) = √(height(cm) × weight(kg) ÷ 3600)
DuBois: BSA = 0.007184 × weight^0.425 × height^0.725 (kg and cm).

Example: For someone 170 cm tall who weighs 70 kg, Mosteller gives √(170 × 70 ÷ 3600) = √3.306 = 1.82 m².

Typical BSA Values

The average adult BSA is about 1.7 m², commonly about 1.9 m² for men and 1.6 m² for women. Newborns are near 0.25 m² and children range between that and the adult values.

Worked Example: 170 cm and 70 kg

The Mosteller formula is √(height in cm × weight in kg ÷ 3600). For 170 cm and 70 kg that is √3.306, about 1.82 m². The DuBois formula gives about 1.81 m². The small difference is normal; formulas were derived differently.

Where BSA Is Used

Doctors use body surface area to dose some chemotherapy drugs, and to index measurements such as cardiac output or kidney function to body size. The calculator is for information only; clinical dosing must be set by the treating team.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which BSA formula is best?

Mosteller is the most commonly used because it is simple and gives results close to the others. Hospitals may specify a preferred formula in their protocols.

Why is BSA used for drug dosing?

Metabolism and blood volume scale with body size better than with weight alone, so BSA-based dosing gives more consistent drug exposure for certain medicines.

What is a normal BSA?

About 1.6 to 1.9 m² for adults, depending on size and sex.

Can I use this calculator for children?

The formulas work for children, but pediatric dosing should always be confirmed by a clinician.

Is BSA the same as skin area?

It is an estimate of total skin surface from height and weight, not a direct measurement.

What is a normal body surface area?

Average adults are often cited around 1.7 m² for women and 1.9 m² for men, but it varies with size.

Which BSA formula is best?

Mosteller is the easiest and widely used. Differences between common formulas are typically small for average adults.

Can I use this for medication doses?

No. Doses must be prescribed and checked by a clinician or pharmacist.
