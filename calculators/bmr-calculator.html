About BMR

The Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) represents the number of calories your body burns while at rest to maintain basic physiological functions like breathing, circulation, and cell production.

Mifflin-St Jeor Equation

This calculator uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, which is considered one of the most accurate methods for estimating BMR.
For Men: BMR = 10W + 6.25H - 5A + 5
For Women: BMR = 10W + 6.25H - 5A - 161

Activity Multipliers

To determine total daily energy expenditure (TDEE), the BMR is multiplied by an activity factor:
Sedentary: BMR x 1.2
Lightly active: BMR x 1.375
Moderately active: BMR x 1.465
Active: BMR x 1.55
Very active: BMR x 1.725
Extra active: BMR x 1.9
