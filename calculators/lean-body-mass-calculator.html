What is lean body mass?

Lean body mass (LBM) is your total weight minus the weight of body fat. It includes muscle, bones, organs, skin and body water. Doctors use LBM to dose some medicines, and athletes track it to see whether training is adding muscle.

The three formulas

The Boer, James and Hume equations were each developed from measured data, and they give slightly different answers. Averaging them smooths out the differences. The body fat percentage shown is simply what is left after subtracting lean mass from your weight, so treat it as a rough guide.
