How It Works

This calculator determines your current grade based on the weighted average of your assignments. If you provide a target grade and the weight of your final exam, it calculates the minimum score you need on that exam to reach your goal.

Weighted Grade Formula

Current Grade = (Sum of (Grade × Weight)) / (Sum of Weights)

Required Score Formula

Required = (Target - ((100% - Final Weight) × Current Grade)) / Final Weight
