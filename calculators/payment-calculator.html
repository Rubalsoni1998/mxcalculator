About Payment Calculator

This payment calculator allows you to determine the monthly payments on a fixed-term loan. By entering the loan amount, the term (how long you have to pay it back), and the interest rate, you can see exactly how much you'll pay each month.

How it Works

Most fixed-term loans (like mortgages, auto loans, and personal loans) are amortized. This means your monthly payment stays the same, but the portion going towards interest decreases over time, while the portion going towards the principal (the original loan amount) increases.

Key Terms

  • Loan Amount: The total amount of money you are borrowing.
  • Loan Term: The period of time over which you will repay the loan.
  • Interest Rate: The cost of borrowing money, expressed as a percentage of the loan amount per year.

Amortization

Amortization is the process of spreading out a loan into a series of fixed payments over time. You'll be paying off interest and principal in different amounts each month, even though your total payment remains the same.
