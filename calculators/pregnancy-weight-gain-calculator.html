Weight gain during pregnancy

How much weight is healthy to gain depends mostly on your body mass index (BMI) before pregnancy. People who start underweight are advised to gain more, and people who start with a higher BMI are advised to gain less. Expecting twins raises the recommended range.

Gain is usually slow in the first trimester and then steadier from week 14 on. This calculator shows the total recommended range, the range expected by your current week, and the typical weekly rate for a single baby.

Where the weight goes

The baby, placenta and amniotic fluid account for part of the gain. The rest is increased blood and fluid volume, breast and uterine growth, and stored energy for breastfeeding. Always follow the advice of your doctor or midwife, who can adjust these ranges for your situation.
