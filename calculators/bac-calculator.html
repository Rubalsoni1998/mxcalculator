How BAC is estimated

Blood alcohol concentration (BAC) is the percentage of alcohol in your blood. This calculator uses the Widmark formula: the grams of alcohol you drank, divided by your body weight times a distribution factor (about 0.68 for men and 0.55 for women), minus about 0.015% for each hour that has passed.

What counts as a standard drink?

A standard drink contains a fixed amount of pure alcohol, but the amount differs by country: 14 g in the United States, 10 g in many other countries and 8 g for a UK unit. A typical 12 oz beer, 5 oz glass of wine or 1.5 oz shot of spirits is one US standard drink.

Peak BAC depends on how fast you drink and whether you have eaten. Because the estimate cannot account for these, it can be well off. The only safe level for driving is zero.
