About Concrete Calculation

Calculating the right amount of concrete is crucial for construction projects to avoid waste or shortages. Concrete is typically measured in cubic yards (1 yard = 27 cubic feet).

Formulas Used

  • Rectangular Slab: Length (ft) × Width (ft) × Thickness (ft) = Cubic Feet
  • Round Column: π × r² × Height (ft) = Cubic Feet
  • Bags: 1 cubic foot of concrete is approximately 145-150 lbs.

Tips

It's standard practice to add a safety margin (5-10%) to your calculation to account for spillage, uneven subgrade, or form bending.
