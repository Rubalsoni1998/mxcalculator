About Interest

Interest is the fee you pay for borrowing money or the return you earn on an investment. This calculator helps you determine how much your money can grow over time.

Simple vs. Compound Interest

Simple Interest: Calculated only on the principal amount. If you invest $100 at 5% simple interest for 3 years, you earn $5 each year, totaling $15.

Compound Interest: Calculated on the principal plus any accumulated interest. If you invest $100 at 5% compound interest for 3 years, you earn interest on your interest. Year 1: $5. Year 2: $5.25. Year 3: $5.51. Total: $15.76. Over long periods, compound interest grows wealth significantly faster.

Compounding Frequency

The more frequently interest is compounded, the higher the total return. Daily compounding yields more than annual compounding because the interest is added to the principal more often, allowing it to earn more interest sooner.
