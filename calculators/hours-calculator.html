About Hours Calculator

This calculator helps employees and freelancers track their weekly work hours and estimate gross pay. It handles standard time formats and allows for break deductions per shift.

How it Works

Enter your start and end times for each day. If a shift extends past midnight (e.g., 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM), the calculator automatically detects this and adds 24 hours to the end time for accurate duration calculation.

Decimal Hours

Payroll is often calculated using decimal hours. For example, 8 hours and 30 minutes is equal to 8.5 decimal hours. This conversion happens automatically here.
