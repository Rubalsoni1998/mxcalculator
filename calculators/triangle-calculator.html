About Triangle Calculator

This calculator uses the Law of Sines and the Law of Cosines to solve for the missing sides and angles of a triangle. It can handle various scenarios like SSS (Side-Side-Side), SAS (Side-Angle-Side), ASA (Angle-Side-Angle), and AAS (Angle-Angle-Side).

Laws Used

  • Law of Sines: a/sin(A) = b/sin(B) = c/sin(C)
  • Law of Cosines: c² = a² + b² - 2ab cos(C)
  • Angle Sum: A + B + C = 180°

Area Calculation

The area is calculated using Heron's Formula when all sides are known:
Area = √[s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)], where 's' is the semi-perimeter.
