Salary Calculations

This calculator helps you convert salary amounts between different frequencies. It also allows you to see the impact of unpaid time off (like holidays or vacations if unpaid) on your effective hourly or annual rate.

Unadjusted vs. Adjusted

  • Unadjusted: Assumes a standard 52-week year with no time off. This is typical for salaried employees who get paid holidays and vacation.
  • Adjusted: Subtracts the specified holidays and vacation days from the total working days. This is useful for hourly contractors or employees who do not receive paid time off.
