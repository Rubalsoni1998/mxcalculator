Target heart rate and exercise zones

Your target heart rate is the range of beats per minute that makes exercise effective without overdoing it. Health organizations suggest 50–70% of your maximum heart rate for moderate activity and 70–85% for vigorous activity.

Karvonen method

The Karvonen method adds your resting heart rate to a percentage of your heart rate reserve (maximum minus resting). Because it accounts for fitness through resting pulse, it gives zones that suit fitter and less fit people differently.

To find your resting heart rate, count your pulse for 60 seconds first thing in the morning before getting up.
