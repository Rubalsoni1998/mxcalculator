Conception Calculation

Pregnancy typically lasts about 40 weeks (280 days) from the first day of your last menstrual period (LMP) or 38 weeks (266 days) from the date of conception.

How it Works

This calculator works backward from your due date. By subtracting 266 days from the due date, we estimate the date of conception. This assumes a standard ovulation cycle (occurring around day 14 of a 28-day cycle).

Conception Window

Because ovulation can vary slightly from month to month, conception doesn't always happen on the exact mathematical average. The calculator provides a "window" of likely dates, usually spanning a few days before and after the estimated date.
