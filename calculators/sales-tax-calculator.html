About Sales Tax

Sales tax is a consumption tax imposed by the government on the sale of goods and services. A conventional sales tax is levied at the point of sale, collected by the retailer, and passed on to the government.

How to Calculate

Calculating sales tax is straightforward: multiply the price of the item by the tax rate.
Tax Amount = Price × (Tax Rate / 100)
Total Price = Price + Tax Amount

Tax Rates

Tax rates vary significantly by country, state, and even city. In the United States, for example, there is no federal sales tax, but most states impose their own, often combined with local city or county taxes.
