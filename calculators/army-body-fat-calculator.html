About the Army tape test

The tape test estimates body fat from circumference measurements instead of skinfold calipers or scans. For men it uses height, neck and abdomen; for women it adds the hips. The formula is the same one used in the Navy method, and the limits shown here come from the 2013 version of Army Regulation 600-9.

How to measure

Stand relaxed and measure on bare skin. Keep the tape snug but not tight, and level with the floor. Measure each site three times and use the average. Small measuring errors can change the result by a few percentage points, so repeat the test under the same conditions each time.

Tape methods are less accurate for very muscular or very lean people. Use the result as a screening estimate only.
