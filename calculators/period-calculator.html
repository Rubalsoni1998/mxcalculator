Predicting your next period

A menstrual cycle is counted from the first day of one period to the first day of the next. Most cycles last between 21 and 35 days, and periods usually last three to seven days.

Enter the first day of your last period and your typical cycle length, and this calculator lists your next six periods and the day you are likely to ovulate in each cycle.

Tracking tips

Note the start date every month for several cycles to learn your own average. The more regular your cycles, the more accurate predictions become.
