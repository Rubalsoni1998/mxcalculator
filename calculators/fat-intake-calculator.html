How much fat should you eat?

Fat supplies energy, helps you absorb vitamins A, D, E and K, and is needed to build cells and hormones. For adults, health guidelines suggest getting 20–35% of calories from fat, and keeping saturated fat below 10% of calories.

The calculator estimates your daily calories and converts those percentages into grams. Fat has 9 calories per gram, more than twice protein or carbohydrate, so small servings add up quickly.

Type matters more than total

Replacing saturated fat with unsaturated fat, and avoiding industrial trans fat, is linked to better heart health.
