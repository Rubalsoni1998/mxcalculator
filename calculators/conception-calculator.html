When did conception happen?

Conception takes place when a sperm fertilizes an egg, usually within 12 to 24 hours of ovulation. Because ovulation falls about 14 days before the next period, your last period and cycle length give a good first estimate of the date.

This tool also shows the implantation window, when the fertilized egg attaches to the uterus, and the earliest date a home test is likely to turn positive.

Due dates

Pregnancy is dated from the first day of your last period, so a full-term birth is about 40 weeks from that day, or about 38 weeks from conception. If your cycle is longer or shorter than 28 days, the due date is adjusted to match.
