What is body surface area?

Body surface area (BSA) is the total area of your skin, measured in square metres. It correlates with metabolic rate better than weight alone, so it is used to dose chemotherapy, to index kidney function and heart output, and to assess burns.

Formulas

Mosteller is the simplest: the square root of height in centimetres times weight in kilograms, divided by 3,600. DuBois and DuBois (1916) is the classic reference. Haycock and Gehan-George were developed using data that included children, and Fujimoto was derived from Japanese subjects.
