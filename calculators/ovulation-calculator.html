How ovulation is estimated

Ovulation is when an ovary releases an egg. In a typical 28-day cycle it happens around day 14, but the real timing depends on your cycle length. This calculator counts back from your expected next period using your luteal phase, which is usually about 14 days.

Your fertile window

Sperm can survive for up to five days, while an egg lives for about a day. That is why the fertile window runs from five days before ovulation through the day after, with the two or three days before ovulation being most fertile.

If your cycles vary by more than a few days, the estimate will be less accurate. A doctor can help if you have been trying to conceive for a year (six months if over 35).
