The five common body shapes

Hourglass: bust and hips are about equal with a narrow waist. Pear: hips are wider than the bust. Inverted triangle: the bust or shoulders are wider than the hips. Rectangle: bust, waist and hips are similar. Apple: the waist is as wide as, or wider than, the bust and hips.

How the result is worked out

The calculator compares your three measurements. Bust and hips within about 5% of each other are treated as balanced, and a waist at least a quarter smaller than the larger of the two gives an hourglass. Real bodies sit on a spectrum, so you may fall between two shapes.

The waist-to-hip ratio is also shown. A ratio of 0.85 or higher in women is associated with greater health risk in World Health Organization guidance.
