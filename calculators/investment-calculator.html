Investment Calculator

This calculator helps you determine how much your investment will be worth in the future, given a return rate, contribution amounts, and compounding frequency.

Return Rate

The rate of return is the profit on an investment over a period of time, expressed as a proportion of the original investment. For example, if you invest $1,000 and it grows to $1,100 in one year, your return rate is 10%.

Compounding

Compounding is the process where interest is credited to an existing principal amount as well as to interest already paid. It essentially means "interest on interest". The more frequent the compounding, the greater the final return.

Contributions

Regular contributions to an investment account can significantly increase the final value. Dollar-cost averaging, or investing a fixed dollar amount at regular intervals, is a common strategy to build wealth over time.
