Date Calculation

The Date Calculator allows you to calculate the number of days between two dates, or find a date in the past or future by adding or subtracting years, months, weeks, and days.

Calendar System

This calculator uses the Gregorian calendar, which is the most widely used civil calendar in the world. It accounts for leap years (occurring every 4 years, except for years divisible by 100 but not by 400).
