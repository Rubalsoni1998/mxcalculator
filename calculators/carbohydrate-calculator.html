How many carbs do you need?

Carbohydrates are the body's main fuel, especially for the brain and for exercise. Dietary guidelines suggest that 45–65% of your calories come from carbohydrate, and that adults get at least 130 g per day.

This calculator estimates your daily calories from your age, sex, height, weight, activity and goal, then converts the recommended percentage range into grams. Carbohydrate has 4 calories per gram.

Quality matters

Choose mostly whole grains, legumes, fruit, vegetables and dairy, which bring fiber and nutrients, and keep added sugars low. A fiber target of about 14 g per 1,000 calories is shown for reference.
