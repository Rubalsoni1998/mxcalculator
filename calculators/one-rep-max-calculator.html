What is a one-rep max?

Your one-rep max (1RM) is the heaviest weight you can lift for a single repetition with good form. Because testing a true max is tiring and carries injury risk, many lifters estimate it from a lighter set done to near failure.

Formulas used

Epley, Brzycki, Lander, Lombardi, Mayhew, O'Conner and Wathan each predict 1RM from the weight and the reps. They agree closely for low reps and drift apart for high reps, so this calculator shows all seven and their average.

Use the percentage chart to plan sets. For example, strength work is often done at 80–90% of 1RM, while muscle-building sets commonly use 65–80%.
