About Interest Rates

This calculator helps you determine the annual interest rate of a loan when you know how much you borrowed, how long the loan is for, and what your monthly payments are.

Why Calculate the Rate?

Sometimes lenders advertise "low monthly payments" but extend the loan term or hide fees, resulting in a much higher effective interest rate. Calculating the rate yourself helps you compare different loan offers apples-to-apples.

APR (Annual Percentage Rate)

The rate calculated here is essentially the APR if there are no other upfront fees involved. It represents the true annual cost of borrowing money.
