What is GFR?

The glomerular filtration rate (GFR) measures how much blood your kidneys filter each minute. A healthy young adult has an eGFR of about 100 mL/min/1.73 m², and it declines slowly with age.

Because measuring GFR directly is complicated, labs estimate it from the creatinine level in your blood, along with your age and sex. This calculator uses the 2021 CKD-EPI equation, which no longer includes a race factor, and also shows the older MDRD equation for comparison.

Understanding the stages

Stages G1 to G5 describe how much function is reduced. A single low reading does not mean you have kidney disease: dehydration, intense exercise, high meat intake and some medicines can raise creatinine temporarily.
