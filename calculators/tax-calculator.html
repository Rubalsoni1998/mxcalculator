About Income Tax

This calculator estimates your federal income tax liability based on the selected tax year's brackets and standard deductions. It accounts for filing status and taxable income after deductions.

Marginal vs. Effective Tax Rate

  • Marginal Tax Rate: The tax rate paid on your *last* dollar of income. This is your "tax bracket."
  • Effective Tax Rate: The average rate you pay on your *total* income after deductions. This is usually lower than your marginal rate because of the progressive tax system.

Standard Deduction

The standard deduction is a specific dollar amount that reduces the income you're taxed on. Your standard deduction varies according to your filing status.
