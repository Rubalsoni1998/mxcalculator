About Compound Interest

Compound interest is the concept of earning interest on your investment, then earning interest on your investment plus the interest you've already earned. This "snowball effect" allows your money to grow faster over time compared to simple interest.

Compound Frequency

The frequency at which interest is added to your principal makes a difference. Daily compounding will yield a higher return than annual compounding because the interest is added to the account more often, allowing it to start earning more interest sooner.
