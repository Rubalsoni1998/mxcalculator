What is Ideal Body Weight (IBW)?

The term "ideal body weight" is used to describe a weight range that is considered healthy for a person of a specific height. It is important to note that no single formula is perfect, as they do not account for muscle mass, bone density, or body composition.

Formulas Used

  • Robinson (1983): Modified the Devine formula and is widely used.
  • Miller (1983): Often gives lower estimates than other formulas.
  • Devine (1974): Originally for drug dosing, became a standard for IBW.
  • Hamwi (1964): One of the earliest formulas, often used in clinical settings ("106 lbs for first 5 feet...").
  • Healthy BMI: Based on the World Health Organization's recommendation of a BMI between 18.5 and 25.

Limitations

These calculations are general guidelines. They are not suitable for athletes, bodybuilders, pregnant women, or the elderly, where muscle mass or other factors significantly influence weight.
