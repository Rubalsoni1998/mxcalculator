About Scientific Calculators

A scientific calculator is a type of electronic calculator, usually but not always handheld, designed to calculate problems in science, engineering, and mathematics. They have completely replaced slide rules in traditional applications, and are widely used in both education and professional settings.

Functions Included

  • Trigonometry: Sine (sin), Cosine (cos), Tangent (tan) and their inverses (asin, acos, atan).
  • Logarithms: Natural Log (ln) and Log base 10 (log).
  • Exponentiation: Square root (√), Square (x²), and Power (x^y).
  • Constants: Pi (π) and Euler's number (e).

Angle Modes

DEG (Degrees): A full circle is divided into 360 degrees. Most common in elementary geometry.
RAD (Radians): A full circle is 2π radians. Standard unit of angular measure in advanced mathematics and calculus.
