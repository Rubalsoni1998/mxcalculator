How is Age Calculated?

The age of a person can be counted differently in different cultures. This calculator uses the most common system in Western countries: age increases on a person's birthday. For example, a person who has lived for 3 years and 11 months is 3 years old, and will turn 4 on their next birthday.

Time Units

While years are the most common unit, understanding age in total months, weeks, or even days can be useful for medical, developmental, or curiosity reasons.
