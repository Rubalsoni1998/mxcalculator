Pregnancy Calculation

Estimated due dates are calculated by adding 280 days (40 weeks) to the first day of the last menstrual period (assuming a 28-day cycle). This method assumes that conception occurred approximately 14 days after the start of the last period.

Calculation Methods

  • Last Period: The most common method. Adds 40 weeks to the start of the last cycle.
  • Conception Date: If you know exactly when you conceived, the due date is 266 days (38 weeks) from that date.
  • IVF: Calculated based on the transfer date and the age of the embryo (3-day or 5-day).
  • Ultrasound: Calculated by projecting forward from the estimated gestational age determined by the doctor.

Trimesters

  • First Trimester: Week 1 through Week 12
  • Second Trimester: Week 13 through Week 26
  • Third Trimester: Week 27 to birth
