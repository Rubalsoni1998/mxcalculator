What is a Finance Calculator?

This calculator performs standard Time Value of Money (TVM) calculations involving present value, future value, payments, interest rate, and number of periods. It is useful for mortgage, loan, investment, annuity, and other financial calculations.

Key Variables

  • N (Number of Periods): The total number of payment periods (e.g., 60 months for a 5-year loan).
  • I/Y (Interest Rate): The annual interest rate.
  • PV (Present Value): The current value of a sum of money or the initial loan amount. (Cash inflow is positive, cash outflow is negative).
  • PMT (Payment): The amount paid or received in each period.
  • FV (Future Value): The value of the cash flow at the end of the periods.

Sign Convention

This calculator follows the standard cash flow sign convention:
Positive (+) numbers represent money received (inflow).
Negative (-) numbers represent money paid out (outflow).
Example: For a loan, you receive the Principal (+PV), and you make Payments (-PMT).
