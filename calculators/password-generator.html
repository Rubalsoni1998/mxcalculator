About Password Security

A strong password is your first line of defense against unauthorized access to your digital life. Here are some tips for creating secure passwords:

  • Length is key: Longer passwords are exponentially harder to crack. Aim for at least 12-16 characters.
  • Variety: Use a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols to increase entropy.
  • Avoid patterns: Do not use common words, birthdays, or keyboard sequences (e.g., "qwerty", "123456").
  • Uniqueness: Never reuse passwords across different sites. Use a password manager to keep track of them.
