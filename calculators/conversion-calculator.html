About Unit Conversion

Unit conversion involves changing the value of a physical quantity from one unit to another, while preserving the same quantity. This is essential in science, engineering, and daily life where different systems (Metric vs. Imperial) are used.

Common Systems

  • Metric System (SI): Based on powers of 10. Used by most of the world (Meters, Kilograms, Celsius).
  • Imperial/US Customary System: Based on historical definitions. Used primarily in the US (Feet, Pounds, Fahrenheit).
