How calories burned are estimated

A MET (metabolic equivalent of task) describes how hard an activity is compared with resting. Sitting quietly is 1 MET, and running at 6 mph is about 9.8 METs. Multiply the MET value by your weight in kilograms and the time in hours to estimate calories used.

Heavier people burn more calories for the same activity because they move more mass. The table shows the same duration for other activities so you can compare, for example, a walk against a swim.

Limits

MET values are averages from studies. Heart-rate monitors and fitness trackers can refine the figure for you, but all methods are estimates.
