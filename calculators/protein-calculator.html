How much protein do you need?

The Recommended Dietary Allowance for adults is 0.8 grams of protein per kilogram of body weight, which is enough to prevent deficiency in most healthy people. Needs are higher when you train hard, are older, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or are cutting calories and want to keep muscle.

Spreading it out

Muscle responds well to moderate protein servings across the day, roughly 20–40 g per meal for most people. Good sources include eggs, dairy, fish, poultry, lentils, beans, tofu and nuts.
